Injury Tracking & Depth Deficiency

Tracks injuries and the deficiency created when starters are replaced by 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups, on both offense and defense, and turns that into a line signal. The table below is the verified Week 10 injury report from the official NFL.com source, with per-player official links.

Point-in-time snapshot. Injury designations change weekly and are finalized near game time. The table below reflects the official NFL.com Week 10 injury report as fetched on 2026-11-14. "String" depth (starter / 2nd / 3rd / 4th) is a label derived from the official depth-chart order and is flagged where the official page does not explicitly label string depth (see Flagged for Review).

Methodology

  1. Capture — pull the official injury report: player, position, unit, injury, practice status, and game status.
  2. Map to depth — cross-reference the official depth chart to determine whether a missing starter is replaced by a 2nd, 3rd, or 4th-string backup.
  3. Assess unit impact — evaluate the position group affected (e.g., offensive line, secondary, pass rush) and its role in the game plan.
  4. Quantify — translate a backup-forced unit into an expected-performance adjustment (in points/EPA); this is a planned signal, see Q5.
  5. Apply to lines — feed the adjustment into the model line for the affected team (both offense and defense).
