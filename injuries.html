What the designations mean, according to the league

These are the league's own definitions, not ours:

  • Practice status — Did Not Participate; Limited Participation (less than 100% of a player's normal repetitions); Full Participation (100%).
  • Game status — Out (will not play); Doubtful (unlikely to participate); Questionable (uncertain). The former Probable category was removed because roughly 95% of players listed as Probable did in fact play.
  • A player certain to play is removed from the game status report entirely, even if he appeared in the practice report that week. A blank game status on this table therefore means the club did not flag him, not that the data is missing.

Source: NFL.com — Competition Committee injury report revisions ↗ and the current report at nfl.com/injuries ↗.

We deliberately do not convert a designation into a probability of playing. Doing so requires historical play rates by designation, which needs a results feed no official source provides (Q-12, Q-13). An invented number here would look like evidence and would not be.
