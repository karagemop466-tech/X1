A strategy earns nothing until it clears every blocking gate. The gates were written down before any result was scored; changing one after seeing results is recorded as a violation rather than absorbed silently.

Which strategies clear them today

16 strategies are scored. Those with a 95% confidence interval entirely above zero: S_wind_under.

But a confidence interval is a per-test statement. With 16 tests in the family, the threshold for any one of them to be credible is p ≤ 0.003125, and 0 strategies clear it. That is the gate doing its job.

Gate G0 is the one that binds everything

Provenance requires licensed inputs with timestamps, no lookahead, a recorded settlement rule, and a hashed model version. Prices fail the first requirement, so every economic result is blocked at G0 regardless of how good the numbers look. This is why the leaderboard can be busy and still have zero eligible entries.
