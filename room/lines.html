Originate the number

Our line, the market line, and the gap

The order is deliberate: side first, then total, then moneyline. That is the order in which a book builds a number, and it is the order in which errors compound — a wrong side propagates into a wrong total and a wrong price. The fair line, the market line and the difference are published as three separate columns so the disagreement can be seen rather than summarised.

What the gap column does and does not mean

A positive spread gap means our number is further from zero than the market's on the away side. It is not an edge. An edge requires a price, and the market column here is a documented third-party benchmark, not a licensed quote with a timestamp. Read it as "we disagree with the benchmark by this much", which is a testable claim, and not as "we would have made this much".
