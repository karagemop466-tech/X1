What this table is telling you

Sorted by what blocks the most: M-01 (prices) blocks every economic result in the project and is the only one that requires spending money. M-02 is free and already automated — it only needs the week to progress. M-04 (snaps) is what makes "2nd string" a measurement instead of a guess; without it, availability work stays at named starters, which the league does publish.

Every row that says "low" is already partly built: the capture machinery, the hash retention and the scheduler exist. What does not exist is the data behind them.
