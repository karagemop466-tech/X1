The choice that actually matters

S-01 against S-02. Everything else is housekeeping. Either the room stays honest and quiet until a price source exists and one strategy survives correction, or a price source is licensed and the whole thing becomes evaluable within a season. Both are defensible. What is not defensible is publishing a leaderboard winner off a backtest, which is why S-01 is ranked above S-02 rather than below it.
