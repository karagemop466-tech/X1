“2nd string” requires a depth order. The league publishes no league-wide depth chart; clubs publish their own, dated inconsistently, sometimes as an image, sometimes with jersey numbers in the name column, sometimes with two players sharing one slot. No official snap-count feed is licensed here.

So the room does not publish a depth rank for any player. It publishes named starters where the league names them, and leaves the rest blank. The measured substitute — snaps — is listed as gap M-04 on Missing information.
