One limit, stated plainly

The sandbox this room was built in could reach www.nfl.com only through a page-fetch tool, not by direct request. That means the transcription is a careful reading, not an automated scrape, and the defence against a misread cell is the arithmetic on Staleness ledger: league closure, identity checks and cross-page agreement all have to hold at once before a value is published.
