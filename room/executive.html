Executive summary

The project in one page

What was built, what it proves, what it cannot prove, and what one decision would change.

1. What this is

A lab for originating our own NFL lines and for deciding honestly whether those lines are any good. The output of a round is not a pick list; it is a measurement with an interval attached, published whether or not it flatters the model.

2. What the measurement says today

0strategies eligible to stake
1clear p ≤ 0.05 before correction
0clear it after correction
0.80false positives expected by chance from 16 tests

The originated line beats a naive constant and loses to the market benchmark. The one strategy with a credible-looking interval does not survive being tested alongside fifteen others. That is the honest state of the project, and it is the state a good gate produces.

3. What changed this round

  1. The site stopped printing a broken label. Every page's navigation rendered a literal & because a label was escaped twice; 94 occurrences in 29 files, fixed at the generator and now guarded at build time.
  2. Week 11 availability exists. 253 official injury rows across 24 clubs were read live on the first game day of the week. Only 16 carry a game designation.
  3. Three named players were checked against the articles that name them. Two qualified as announced-starter evidence; one (Brissett) is a reported trade and is left blank rather than upgraded.
  4. The staleness was measured, not assumed. 104 of 192 published standings cells have moved since the snapshot.
  5. The multiplicity correction was computed. 16 rules, a corrected bar of 0.003125, and 0 survivors.

4. What blocks a decision

Three things, in order of what they unlock:

  1. A price source (M-01). Without licensed, timestamped quotes no ROI can be evaluated, and every economic number stays research-only.
  2. Time (M-08). The pre-registered shadow baseline needs a scored window; no amount of code shortens it.
  3. Final designations and inactives (M-02, M-03). The injury report is readable today but is not yet final for 30 of 32 clubs.

5. The one decision worth making now

Choose between S-01 (stay quiet until a strategy survives correction) and S-02 (license a price feed and make everything evaluable). Every other item on the suggestions board is housekeeping. S-01 is recommended because it is consistent with the gate that was registered before any result was seen.

6. How to check any of this

  1. Run python3 tools/verify_2026_11_19.py — eight checks, all rebuild from the committed transcriptions.
  2. Open Staleness ledger and follow any row's source link to the official page.
  3. Open Flagged for review — everything this round could not verify is listed there with its source.
