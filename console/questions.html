Q-01 Can the model in this repository originate a line that beats the closing market? ANSWERED - and the answer is no
Area
Signal
Information we hold
The market's own closing spread correlates 0.434 with the actual home margin (n=6,102). Our walk-forward model correlates 0.2332 (n=6,102). On the side our fair line misses the actual margin by 14.198 points RMSE against the market's 13.14.
Information we do not hold
Player-level participation and snap counts, real-time injury timing, book-specific prices, and any measure of where the line opened. None of these exist in a source available to this repository.
Answer
No, not on the evidence available. The model is roughly one point per game worse than the closing line it is trying to beat, and every strategy built on it loses money before staking is considered.
How to discover the rest
Close the gaps in SG-02 and SG-08, then re-run the gates. Do not raise the bet threshold and call the remainder skill.

Q-04 Are the side and the total in the same game correlated? Does that make parlays mispriced? ANSWERED
Area
Parlays
Information we hold
Phi = 0.0199 (n=6,102, 95% CI -0.0052 to 0.0449). Spread error against total error in the same game is essentially the same story.
Information we do not hold
Book-specific parlay prices; every book discounts parlays differently and none publishes it in a source we hold.
Answer
The two legs are close enough to independent that the product rule is not far off. A two-leg -110 parlay therefore needs a 27.44% joint probability to break even, and the extra leg multiplies the vig rather than creating value.
How to discover the rest
Only a book's real parlay price would change this, and it would make parlays worse, not better.

Q-06 Is a rest advantage worth points? ANSWERED - yes, but too small to bet
Area
Signal

Information we do not hold

Q-09 Can a parlay strategy ever pass the gates? ANSWERED
Area
Parlays
Information we hold
ST-PARLAY-4 placed 889 bets out of sample and returned -36.5% flat. Measured correlation between the two legs is 0.0199 (n=6,102).
Information we do not hold
Nothing material.
Answer
Not with these legs. A parlay multiplies the vig on each leg and needs both legs to be right; with a signal that has no measurable edge on either leg, the combination is strictly worse than the singles.
How to discover the rest
Only a genuine edge on a single leg would change this.

Q-15 Does a bigger disagreement with the market mean a better bet? ANSWERED - no
Area
Signal
Information we hold
Point-biserial correlation between edge size and bet result, measured per strategy and published with n on the correlations page. Every credible interval covers zero, and the largest side-edge threshold is significantly negative.
Information we do not hold
Nothing material.
Answer
No. Raising the threshold just reduces the sample; it does not select better bets. This is the clearest evidence that the disagreement is model error rather than market error.
How to discover the rest
Nothing. If a future model produces a positive value here with a tight interval, that is the signal to promote it.
