A stored forecast is a probability plus the inputs that produced it. All 52 rows of the frozen Week 11 record are listed with their input vector, and 26 of 26 in-scope published probabilities re-derive exactly from those inputs.

The constants are read from the pre-registered model by import, not retyped, so this table cannot drift from PR-01 without failing. The tolerance is half a unit of the last published digit (5×10-7), because the record stores six decimal places. Total rows publish a line rather than a margin probability, so they sit outside this check by design and say so; their re-derivation compares the published line with the corpus league average.

No market number is stored anywhere in these rows. The price policy keeps that column blank for every 2026 fixture, which is open question Q-02 — a residual against the market, and therefore closing-line value, cannot be computed at all until that is settled.

Every row: the frozen record's hash, the registration, and the two official page URLs it was read from, in the TSV.
