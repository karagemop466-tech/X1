Removing luck means publishing an interval, not a win rate. 1 settled rows of the 66-row escrow ledger are scoreable today, 0 of them from Week 11; 65 rows wait on official finals.

Skill here is Brier score measured against two benchmarks — a coin, and the 0.55 home-team baseline that any NFL side pick starts from — with a bootstrap that resamples whole games. The cluster matters: the escrow stores three or four markets per fixture, and treating them as independent observations would roughly halve the interval and turn noise into a finding. "Games needed" is how many settled games it would take before the lower bound of that interval clears zero.

Controls registered as PR-02: every pick replaced by a coin flip, and every pick replaced by the market favourite, on the same games.
