What is deliberately not here

Venues are not published on this page except the Munich international game, because the weekly schedule page prints a venue only for that game. Per-fixture venues live on each game centre page and remain the R-24 gap. Kickoff times and networks are blank where this read did not capture them — the 2026-11-16 pack transcribes its own read of the same page and is linked row by row. Nothing is filled in from memory.
