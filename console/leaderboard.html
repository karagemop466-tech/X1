Signal and staking are two different questions and this table keeps them apart. SIGNAL score is computed at flat one unit per bet, so no staking plan can move it: it measures whether the selection itself has value. STAKING score measures what the money management did with that selection (quarter Kelly, capped at 5% of a 100-unit bankroll). Promotion needs signal_score ≥ 55 and all eight gates, so a strategy can top the staking column and still not be promoted.
Why this matters, with the numbers on this board: ST-TOT-5 and ST-ROADFAV have the identical signal score of 25.0, yet they sit 10.2 points apart on staking (35.2 against 25.0). A leaderboard ranked on staking would order them; the signal column says they are the same bet. Promotion requires the signal score, so neither is promoted.
