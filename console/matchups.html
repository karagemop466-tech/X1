What this page is

Each club's own official depth chart supplies the listed starter, the shared-slot flag and the named replacement with the club's own depth rank. The official NFL injury report supplies each listed player's game status. The two are joined by club+player. A defensive slot is described the same way, and the defender's EXPECTED depth is 1 unless his club's listed starter is Out, in which case it is the depth rank of the club's next listed name. No value is inferred, no missing starter is priced in points, and no depth order is derived from jersey number, experience or salary.

The one assumption, stated: The offence-to-defence position mapping in MATCHUPS is football structure, not a measurement: WR<-CB/S, TE<-LB/S, T<-EDGE/DE, G/C<-DT/NT. It is the only modelling assumption in this artefact and is printed on the page so a reviewer can disagree with it.

  • Houston publishes its depth chart as an image and no authorized structured source was used, so Houston appears in no row. Fixtures involving Houston are INCOMPLETE, not empty.
  • The injury report is the 2026-11-17 read (347 rows). It carries a game status for only those players the club listed; absence from the report is not evidence of health.
  • A slot where the club lists more than one player at rank 1 is flagged shared and is not treated as a named starter.
  • Reserve-list status (injured reserve, PUP) is not captured, so a starter who is out for the season may simply be absent from the depth chart's rank 1.
