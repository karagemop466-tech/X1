Houston: an image is not a source

Depth Chart | Houston Texans - HoustonTexans.com — heading "Unofficial Depth Chart". The page renders the depth chart as one image (https://static.clubs.nfl.com/image/upload/texans/a5or0zowuo36y1txf4ly). There is no table, no list and no alt text in the served page: the only text on the page is the heading and the club's headline feed.

Decision: Not transcribed. An image is an official publication but not a machine-readable source; reading it by eye would introduce exactly the transcription risk the rest of this repository exists to remove.

To close it: An authorized structured feed (club CMS export, league data licence) or a text-bearing depth page. Until one exists Houston has 0 of 897 published depth slots and every Houston availability statement is unverifiable. houstontexans.com depth chart

What this means for the deficiency register

  • 31 of 32 clubs have a parsed chart. Houston does not. That is a coverage hole, not a finding of health: the register is silent on Houston and silence must never be read as "no deficiency".
  • Jersey numbers are stripped for joining and never used for ordering. The brief rejects deriving depth from jersey number, experience or salary; nothing in this pipeline uses them. The number is evidence of the club's print format, not of rank.
  • Every club chart is marked "Unofficial". Green Bay prints the date it means: "Unofficial, Nov. 10, 2026". A depth row is therefore a dated club self-report — it must carry the club's date, not the date we fetched it.
