The headline, stated first. Nothing in this repository currently beats the closing NFL line, and the console is built to say so rather than to suggest otherwise. 16 pre-registered strategies were evaluated out of sample over 2004–2026. 0 passed the gates. The best signal score on the board is 31 against a promotion floor of 55. That is the result, and the gates are working as intended.

What this console is

A line-origination and strategy lab for NFL sides, totals, moneylines, parlays and player props. It originates its own lines in a fixed order — side, then total, then moneyline — publishes its fair number next to the market's number and the difference between them, and scores every strategy on two separate axes so that staking cannot disguise a bad signal. Every published row carries a source link. Where no source exists, the cell is blank and the item is on the flagged page.

What is actually in place

  • A verified results feed — 7,426 settled games, reconciled 82/160 against the league's own standings. Results feed.
  • A price-snapshot store — 18,972 rows; both opening and closing numbers present for 1,072 of them, all in 2021. Every other market has one published number and a blank where the other should be. Price store.
  • Originated lines with three columns — model fair, market, difference, for 6,237 out-of-sample games plus the 29 fixtures that had not kicked off when the site was built. Originated lines.
  • Signal and staking scored separately — two columns, two scores, promotion on the signal one. Leaderboard.
  • Replacement depth, not just replacement — 897 slots across 31 clubs, each naming whether the 2nd, 3rd or 4th string steps in. Replacement depth.
  • Correlations with n — 20 published values, each with its sample size and a 95% interval. Correlations.
  • Week 10 closed and reconciled — all 14 finals officially read on 2026-11-17, and the 150-game corpus reproduces all 32 league standings rows exactly (32/32). Week 10 finals.
  • A demotion gate — six rules that send a live strategy back to paper, with thresholds and windows. Gates.
  • A decision register and a suggestion board — 15 questions and 15 suggestions, each with the information we hold, the information we do not, and the paths forward. Questions · Suggestions.

What is not in place, and why

No source available to this repository publishes opening betting prices, player-prop lines, or Houston's depth chart. Those three gaps are the reason some columns are blank. They are listed on the flagged page with their sources, and each has a suggestion attached.

Where the numbers come from

Every official-source register is compared cell by cell against a dated transcription that is re-derived on each build — 859 comparisons in the current verification ledger, and the ledger states the date of each read. One difference is published rather than passed: the 2026 standings table is a 2026-11-14 snapshot that the league has moved past by 262 of 512 cells. Everything else is a fingerprinted third-party mirror of league data and is labelled mirror wherever it appears. Nothing is quoted from memory.
