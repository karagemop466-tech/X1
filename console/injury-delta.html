The brief asks for injury as surprise, not level: what moved between two official reads of the league hub, not who is listed today. 8 of 8 retained reads parsed into a 8-row series, and the diff found 0 movements between adjacent reads.

A zero is a real result and is reported as one: nothing moved between the reads this repository holds, and the reads span 3 calendar day(s) (2026-11-15, 2026-11-17, 2026-11-18). Two of the five are three minutes apart. So this ledger is equally a measurement of the sampling, and the cadence verdict is part of the claim — a status change that both reads miss is invisible to any parser. The decision that follows is SG-17.

No movements to list. The schema is one row per (club, player, field) whose value changed, appeared or disappeared between two adjacent reads — and it is empty because nothing did. The rule recorded in the coverage file: the delta is the signal; the level is only a count inside series.csv.
