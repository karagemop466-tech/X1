What this page is. One row per club per week, carrying only features that a published schedule field can be pointed at: the printed date, the printed slot, the printed network, the printed venue, and the rest interval that falls out of two printed dates. Where the league prints nothing, the cell is blank and says so. That is the gate on the requirement — "built only from published schedule facts" — and it is why two features are published as unavailable rather than estimated.

The rest-day shape of the slate

Counted in whole days between printed dates, over both weeks in this pack. The four-day rows are the Thursday-night pattern; the six-day rows are the two clubs that played Monday night and go again Sunday afternoon; the fourteen-day rows are the clubs coming off a bye. This distribution is the reason the feature exists: a totals model that ignores rest treats four of these club-rows like the thirty-three that got a normal week.

Definitions and limits, in the words the builder writes

  • rest days printed kickoff date minus the club's previous printed fixture date, in days
  • short week rest_days <= 4 (the Thursday-night pattern this feature exists to catch)
  • came off bye no fixture in the immediately previous week
  • prime time slot the block heading the fixture sits under on the schedule page
  • rest hours approximate: measured to midnight UTC of the previous fixture date, because weeks 1-9 are recorded as dates, so the value carries up to a day of slack
  • international the printed venue/event label is not a US state address
  • kickoff utc printed ET slot + 5 h (EST, no DST in November 2026)

Week 11 venues are blank for 12 of 13 fixtures the by-week page prints a location only for the international fixture. Per-fixture venues require the game-centre pass that data/nfl/venues_2026_week10.csv exists for in Week 10 (R-24, PARTIAL).

Kickoff times are read from the rendered page in its default (Eastern) presentation; the page's LOCAL toggle was not exercised, so a reader in another zone sees different strings for the same instant.

rest_days is a date difference, not a game-clock difference, because weeks 1-9 fixtures are recorded with a date and not a printed slot in this repository.

back_to_back_prime is published as 'unmeasured' on every row the Week 10 finals file carries a prime-time note for 3 of 14 games and the weeks 1-9 reads print none, so the feature would be built on a partial field. The field is kept in the schema so a reader can see that it was measured as unavailable, not forgotten.
