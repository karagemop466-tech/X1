Conventions, established rather than assumed

  • Spread sign. A positive spread means the home team is favoured, and the home side covers when the home margin exceeds the spread. Checked over 7,413 settled games: the mean of (margin − spread) is +0.06 and the home cover rate is 48.9%.
  • Cross-check. The correlation between the printed spread and the no-vig home win probability implied by the quoted moneylines is 0.988 over 5,458 games.
  • Opening-line sign. The opening file prints the number as it appears on the board for the named side, so a negative spread means that side is favoured. Converting, the opening home spread is the negative of the board number. Verified over the 272 games of 2021: that reading gives a mean error of −0.39 points and a correlation of 0.405 with the actual margin; the opposite reading gives +3.82 and −0.405.
  • Market calibration. Every season from 1999 to 2026 was checked individually. The mean of (margin − spread) stays inside ±1.9 points in all 28 seasons and the spread/margin correlation stays between 0.33 and 0.51, so the benchmark we are failing is a real one.

What is deliberately not in the deploy gate

The comparison against today's snapshot is a point-in-time check. After a data refresh the pipeline moves on and the comparison would report a freshness difference rather than an error, so it is published as a ledger and not wired into the build gate. The link and source checks are in the gate.
