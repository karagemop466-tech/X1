Nothing on this page has been built ahead of a decision. Each suggestion gives two or three paths with a cost, an effort, a risk and what it unlocks, plus a recommendation. Every row is proposed until a path is chosen and recorded here. That is the point of the page: it is a review queue, not a work log.

SG-01 Get a timestamped, book-identified price feed

proposed

The store can record opening and closing numbers, but no source available here publishes both. One number per market, no book, no timestamp.

Area Prices · Unlocks Q-02, Q-08, Q-12 and the entire demotion gate
A. Licensed odds feed
Subscribe to a commercial historical and live odds API that publishes per-book opening and closing numbers with timestamps.
Cost Paid (recurring)Effort Medium integrationRisk LowUnlocks True open/close pairs, real CLV, a working demotion gate, and the injury-to-line test.
B. Scheduled self-capture
A GitHub Actions cron writes a snapshot of publicly displayed prices to the store on a fixed schedule, every day of the week.
Cost FreeEffort MediumRisk Legal and terms-of-service review required before this runsUnlocks Real opening numbers going forward, but no history before it starts.
C. Do nothing and keep the columns blank
Leave the price store as it is: 2021 both-ends, everything else close-only, blanks flagged.
Cost FreeEffort NoneRisk The eight requirements that depend on prices stay unmetUnlocks Nothing.
Recommendation. Path A if there is budget; otherwise path B after a terms-of-service review. Path C is the honest default and is what the site ships with today.

SG-02 Add player participation and snap counts

proposed

The depth register knows who replaces whom, but not how much that slot is used, so a backup left guard and a backup quarterback carry the same weight.

Area Availability · Unlocks Q-07, Q-08 and any injury-weighted model
A. nflverse weekly rosters and snap counts
Ingest the mirror's snap-count tables, fingerprint them, and weight each depth slot by the share of snaps it played.
Cost FreeEffort MediumRisk Third-party mirror, not league-published; must be labelled as suchUnlocks A weighted deficiency score instead of a headcount.
B. Paid participation data
Buy per-play participation from a commercial provider.
Cost PaidEffort Low once contractedRisk LowUnlocks Same as A with better coverage and a licence.
C. Keep publishing the headcount
Leave the deficiency score unweighted and say so on the page.
Cost FreeEffort NoneRisk The score over-weights low-usage slotsUnlocks Nothing new.
Recommendation. Path A. It is free, it is fingerprinted, and it is the difference between a headcount and a model.

SG-03 Timestamp the injury report

A cron workflow fetches the official injury page each afternoon and appends a dated snapshot to the store.
Cost FreeEffort LowRisk LowUnlocks Designation change times, which is the independent variable the injury model needs.
B. Capture twice daily around the market
Same as A but at two fixed times to bracket the trading day.
Cost FreeEffort LowRisk Slightly more storageUnlocks A tighter estimate of when the market learned what.
C. Keep the snapshot as-is
One fetch per build.
Cost FreeEffort NoneRisk Within-week moves stay invisibleUnlocks Nothing new.
Recommendation. Path A. It is a few lines of workflow and it is the only way Q-08 ever becomes answerable.

Cost Depends on SG-01Effort Low once prices existRisk LowUnlocks The single best available answer to 'is the signal skilled'.
B. Partial CLV on 2021 only
Use the one season where both ends exist to demonstrate the metric.
Cost FreeEffort LowRisk n=264, so it is a demonstration not a proofUnlocks The metric exists and the method is visible even if the sample is thin.
C. Keep using ROI
Leave ROI as the headline.
Cost FreeEffort NoneRisk Luck and staking keep contaminating the signal scoreUnlocks Nothing new.
Recommendation. Path B now so the column exists, path A as soon as prices do. CLV belongs in the signal column, never the staking column.

Cost FreeEffort HighRisk Very large data volumes; the mirror is not league-publishedUnlocks A materially better prior, and the ability to test injuries at the unit level.

Cost FreeEffort MediumRisk LowUnlocks A modest improvement, honestly measured.
C. Keep the current model

Cost FreeEffort NoneRisk The model stays about a point worse than the marketUnlocks Nothing new.
Recommendation. Path B first: it is cheap, and if it does not move the needle the case for A weakens rather than strengthens. Publish the result either way.
