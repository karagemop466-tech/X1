Promotion: paper to live

A strategy earns capital only by passing all eight. Gates G1, G3, G5, G6 and G7 are computed at flat stakes, so they measure the signal. G4 is the only gate that looks at the staking plan, and it cannot be passed on its own.

Re-promotion and the cool-down

  • Re-promotion: A demoted strategy returns to live only after 100 fresh paper bets that pass G1-G8 again on their own.
  • Cool-down: A strategy demoted twice inside one season stays in paper until the next season's out-of-sample window opens.
  • Capital: Paper bets carry no capital. Live bets are capped at the Kelly quarter stake up to 0.05 units, and the cap is not raised after a winning run.
