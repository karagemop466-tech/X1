What this page is. The league posts the injury report several times a week, and the collector in this repository keeps every read it makes — clock time, row count, and a hash over the rows as they were known. This page is what those reads say between them: who arrived on the report, who left it, and which cells moved. The brief asks for the delta rather than the level, so the delta column comes before the counts in every table here, and a state with no earlier read is published as an anchor rather than as a zero.

The states the report has been in

Fourteen reads, three distinct contents. Two of the three are collector reads of the live page; the middle one is the 2026-11-15 hand transcription retained so the series has its middle point. The last state was read twelve times over 28 hours and never moved — that is a stability finding, not a fetch failure, and the two must not be stored as the same thing.

What this series does not yet prove

  • One week deep. The Week 11 report had not been published at the 2026-11-18 probe, so there is no second week to diff. The Wednesday»Thursday»Friday cadence the brief asks for is running, but it has only had Week 10 to run on.
  • No outcome join. A designation change cannot be scored against playing time: the repository holds no inactive lists and no snap counts for 2026, so "was the downgrade real" is unanswerable today and the label stays out of the model. Requirement R-13 stays blocked on exactly that.
  • Two capture methods. One of the three states is a hand transcription. A pair whose methods differ mixes a source change with a method change and is labelled in the table.
