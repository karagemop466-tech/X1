Source registry

What every published number comes from, and what tier of evidence it is.

Four tiers. official is published by the league or by a club on its own site and may be presented as league-verified. mirror is a third-party redistribution of league data that this repository can fingerprint, and it is never called official. computed is arithmetic performed here over the first two, and it must name its inputs. none means no source exists, in which case the cell is blank and flagged.

Why prices carry a warning label

The league publishes no betting lines of any kind. Every price on this site comes from a community mirror of league data, is labelled mirror wherever it appears, and is repeated on the flagged page. It is never presented as league-verified.
