What this table refuses to do

Nothing is inferred. Capacity, roof, surface, attendance, weather and officiating are not printed on the Overview Game Info block of any of these pages, so those cells stay blank with a “not-on-page” flag rather than being filled from a team site or a stadium wiki. The score is blank for the same reason: the page renders it inside elements the text layer prints as “Hidden Score”.

A status is read only from the score box above the Game Info anchor. The word FINAL also appears in a standings table further down the page, and the first version of this runner mistook it for the game status on all 13 unplayed Week 11 fixtures. That is why the parse method for status and score is published as its own column, and why the parser carries a version stamp on every row.
