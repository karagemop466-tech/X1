Joint-Evidence Gate & Official Correlations

Why marginal probability multiplication is barred across multi-leg parlays, SGPs, and player prop combos, backed by official 2026 NFL correlations.

Joint-Probability Law: For binary legs A, B with marginals p_A, p_B and phi correlation rho, P(A and B) = p_A * p_B + rho * sqrt(p_A*(1-p_A)*p_B*(1-p_B)), bounded by Fréchet–Hoeffding limits [max(0, p_A + p_B - 1), min(p_A, p_B)]. Multiplying marginal probabilities is rejected across all combo divisions.
