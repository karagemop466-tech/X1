Strategy Leaderboard — Competition & Rewards Design

A season-long competition between strategies. Strategies earn the right to compete by passing the validation gates; they earn rewards by scoring well against luck-adjusted metrics.

1. Entry rules

1. A strategy = a written rule set + a bet ledger. Every pick is logged before kickoff: timestamp (UTC), strategy ID, market, side, taken line/odds, book, model probability, rationale link. 2. No retroactive entries. A pick not in the ledger before kickoff does not exist. 3. Minimum activity: ≥ 100 closed picks and ≥ 8 active weeks before ROI is displayed publicly (Gate B). 4. Correlated picks (Gate G) count once toward reward scoring.

2. Scoring (proposed weights — awaiting decision OQ-8)

ComponentWeightMetric
CLV score40%mean closing-line value vs consensus close (the luck-removal core)
ROI score30%units won ÷ units risked, on closed picks
Calibration15%Brier score vs book-implied baseline
Stability15%consistency across sub-periods (Gate F)

Composite Score = weighted sum, normalized 0–100. Luck adjustment is structural: CLV (40%) is outcome-independent, calibration punishes overconfident models, and minimum-sample gates keep variance-driven hot streaks from topping the board.

The reward pool (real money vs points vs privileges) is decision OQ-1 — intentionally left open.

4. Anti-gaming rules

  • Line-shopping-only entries (picking+EV from stale lines without a model) are scored on the worst book's close, not the best.
  • Maximum 5 correlated-adjacent picks per slate.
  • Strategies must publish their rule set; changes reset their history (new strategy ID).
  • All ledger rows auditable in-repo (JSON, committed with timestamps).

5. Weekly cadence

Mon: data refresh + flags → Tue: model lines published → Wed: pick ledger committed pre-kickoff → Sun/Mon: games played → Tue: scores updated, digest published (suggestion SG-01).
