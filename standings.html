How this table is verified

Arithmetic consistency check

Run on every refresh, over all 32 rows:

  • Home record + road record must equal the overall W-L-T.
  • Points for − points against must equal net points.
  • Win percentage must match (W + ½T) ÷ games within a small tolerance.

The pass/fail result is shown above the table and is recomputed every run.

Column labels

The 2026 standings page prints its table without a text header row. The page's own LEGEND block defines W, L, T, PCT, HOME, ROAD, DIV, CONF, PF, PA, NET PTS and STRK. The remaining columns — Div %, Conf %, Non-Conf and Last 5 — are positional, and their order is validated by the consistency check rather than by a printed label.
