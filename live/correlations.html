Correlation values

Every value on this page is computed on the spot from the official standings and official team-stats tables this project pulls — not quoted from memory. Each row prints n and the two-sided p-value beside the coefficient and links to both official pages the two columns came from.

What these are. Pearson correlations across the 32 clubs, 2026 regular season to date. A pair is published when |r| ≥ 0.40 or when either side is points for, points against, net points, wins or win percentage. Pairs at |r| ≥ 0.99 are dropped: those are the same statistic printed on two official pages, which is true but carries no modelling information.
What these are not. n = 32 team-seasons is a small sample, so these are weak estimates, and they are team-level associations — they are not the same quantity as the same-game correlation a parlay price needs, and they say nothing about causation. Game-level and player-level correlation cannot be computed from any official source verified so far (Q-10, Q-11, Q-21).

Method

  • Inputs: the official league standings ↗ and the nine official team-stats tables ↗, joined on club.
  • Statistic: Pearson product-moment correlation, r = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / √(Σ(x−x̄)² Σ(y−ȳ)²).
  • Significance: two-sided t-test with n−2 degrees of freedom, t = r√(df / (1−r²)), evaluated with the regularised incomplete beta function.
  • Reproduce it: python3 -m pipeline.model_correlation.
  • The column labelled Lng (longest play) is excluded: the official pages print it with a T suffix for touchdowns, so stripping the suffix would change its meaning.
