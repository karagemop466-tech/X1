Research & signals

Candidate signal families and a disciplined process for originating NFL fair lines. These are hypotheses to test—not established edges or betting recommendations.

Required workflow: freeze information as-of a stated time → produce win/cover/total or prop probability distribution → convert to a fair price → compare with a timestamped, comparable market quote → record decision and eventual outcome. If data licensing, timestamps, market rules, or settlement definitions are unknown, do not score the strategy.

Originating a fair line

  1. Define the exact market and settlement rules. For binary outcomes, estimate probability p; fair decimal odds before margin are 1/p. For spreads/totals/props, produce a calibrated outcome distribution, not just a point estimate.
  2. Use a documented feature cutoff. Store source URL, retrieval time, event time, version, and missingness for each input. Never use information published after the forecast timestamp.
  3. Compare to a market quote with same event, market, side, period, and rules. Track price and line separately; compute expected value only after accounting for payout, vig, and uncertainty.
  4. Log every eligible forecast—including passes—before kickoff. Preserve predictions, model version, odds quote, stake rule, outcome, and correction history immutably.

Parlays and correlation policy

For legs A and B, dependence is measured from joint outcomes: covariance = E[AB] − E[A]E[B], and correlation = covariance / (σAσB), when both variances are nonzero. Estimate on training data only, with uncertainty intervals and relevant conditioning (game, role, weather, game state). Binary-event correlations are sample- and context-sensitive. For larger combinations, use a joint model or simulation validated out of sample. Do not multiply marginal probabilities as if independent unless independence is empirically supported for that defined setting. Correlation alone does not give the parlay's fair payout; the full joint probability and settlement/payout rules are needed.

Irregularity: no odds vendor, historical quote feed, bankroll rules, licensing terms, or market settlement specification exists in the repository. Without these, edge, expected value, and comparative performance cannot be calculated.

Primary sources are linked for traceability; NFL official statistics and injury reports do not themselves establish predictive value or guarantee complete historical availability. Source inventory and current verification boundary are documented in Flagged for Review.
