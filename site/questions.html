How to discover the missing information

Grouped by the kind of gap it is. None of these needs the owner to do research; they need a decision, or a feed.

1. Decisions only the owner can make

Bankroll and stake unit (Q-01), paper or real money (Q-05), reward rules (Q-06), who approves suggestions (Q-25), tie-breaks (Q-27). These have no external source at all. The cheapest way to settle them is to answer them in the suggestions register, where each one is already written as a choice between paths.

2. Data that exists but not from an official public source

Market prices (Q-02, Q-03) and game results (Q-04). Verified as absent from every official page probed: schedule and score pages render client-side and return no table. The options are a licensed feed or the owner's own account export. Until one is chosen, the affected tables stay empty.

3. Data that needs a deeper official source

Play-by-play (Q-21), rest and travel (Q-20), officials (Q-19), weather (Q-18), transactions (Q-17). Each needs a further probe of official endpoints. The probe harness already exists in the pipeline and records what each candidate URL actually returns, so these can be tested without guessing.

4. Quantities that need results data before they can be measured

Injury play rates by designation (Q-12, Q-13), correlation thresholds (Q-10), sample sizes (Q-07). These are not opinions — they are numbers to be estimated once a results feed exists. The gates that depend on them are defined but left unenforced rather than filled with a plausible number.

What the probe actually returned

The pipeline records what every candidate official URL returns, including the HTTP status, the page title and how many server-rendered tables it contains. This is the evidence behind the "no official source" claims on this site.
