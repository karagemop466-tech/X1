Strategies, Gates & Leaderboard

A leaderboard that pays for demonstrated skill — measured against the arithmetic of luck, not vibes.

How the competition removes luck

  • Vig-aware scoring — +10 win / −11 loss at −110: profitable win rate is 52.4%, not 50%.
  • CLV bonus — +2 pts per point of closing-line value: the most luck-resistant skill signal.
  • Measured correlation — same-game φ = -0.023 (n=1,496) is applied to every parlay; undeclared same-game legs are rejected.
  • Gates — 100+ graded bets, binomial significance < 0.05, positive out-of-sample ROI, drawdown cap, portfolio-correlation cap.
