Why this pack exists

The console's standings page published a claim that all 32 clubs and all 16 fields had been “re-read from the league's own standings table on the build date” and matched with no mismatches. That claim was not reproducible. The numbers compared were a constant in the generator, and the table actually audited was a transcription dated 2026-11-14 covering the 137 games played through the Week 10 Thursday game. Twenty-six clubs have played since. The claim is the defect; the fix is a dated read that anyone can re-run, which is what this pack is.

Read at 2026-11-17T22:46Z from https://www.nfl.com/standings/league/2026/REG, https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/REG/week/10. Method: Direct read of the official page, transcribed by hand row by row, then machine-diffed against the collector payload (data/pipeline/*.json, read 2026-11-17T19:49Z) and the 2026-11-14 PSV transcription already in the repository. Where this read and the collector's parser agree, the cell is double-entered; where they disagree, the cell is flagged and neither value is published as verified.
