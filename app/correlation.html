Measured correlation values: strategy-to-strategy, same-game legs, cross-game, and prop families.

SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
Two different quantities, both called correlation. Strategy-to-strategy correlation measures how much two strategies bet the same games the same way — it decides whether a second strategy adds information or is the first one wearing a hat. Same-game leg correlation decides whether a parlay can be priced as if its legs were independent. They are not interchangeable, and this page keeps them apart.
