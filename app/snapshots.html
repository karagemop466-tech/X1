Nothing is back-filled: a stage that was not captured in time keeps its status and an empty value.

Origination order — side first, then total, then moneyline

Publication order is a published column, not a convention. The build refuses to emit a game whose sequences are not exactly 1, 2, 3. The three value columns are kept apart on purpose: fair line is ours, market line is theirs (blank until an official source exists), and difference is fair minus market — which is nothing while the market cell is empty, so it is empty too.

Capture rules

  • open — the number as originated, captured before kickoff.
  • close — the number as published at our cut-off, captured at kickoff.
