Every club's record and points re-derived from the feed match the official standings

The standings page is an independent official quantity: it publishes each club's wins, losses, ties, points for and points against. Recomputing those five numbers from this feed gives 32 clubs x 5 values = 160 checks, 0 mismatches. A single transposed score would break two clubs' sums; none did. The league identity sum(PF) = sum(PA) = 6,291 also holds.

This arithmetic resolves finding I-14. Earlier sessions believed the league standings still showed pre-game records for WAS and NYG after the Thursday game. They do not: WAS 3-5-1 and NYG 5-3-1 are the post-game records. The check passes with the Thursday result included and fails without it (WAS PF 172+24=196). The schedule and the standings never disagreed — the earlier reading did.
