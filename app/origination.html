SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
How the numbers are ordered and compared. Markets are originated in a fixed order — the side first, then the total, then the moneyline — and the builder refuses to emit a later market class before every game has the earlier one. Every row publishes the model's fair line, the market line and the difference as three separate columns. One sign convention throughout: difference = model_fair_line − market_line, in points for sides and totals and in win-probability points (pp) for moneylines. The market column is a third-party mirror value marked UNVERIFIED: no official or licensed source publishes prices (Q-02).

One graded game so far — the machinery, honestly shown

The feed has one completed Week 10 game: WAS 24 at NYG 21. On our own fair side (WAS −0.5) the ticket wins; on our fair total (42.5) the game lands 45, so the under loses. That grading is computable now — the results feed supplies the score. What is still missing is a verified price at which the bet was placed (Q-02), so ROI and closing line value remain blank rather than computed against a mirror number. The moment a verified price feed is attached, this page's columns and the leaderboard fill without any further plumbing.
