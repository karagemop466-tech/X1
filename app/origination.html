Origination and the price-snapshot store

Side, then total, then moneyline — with fair line, market line and difference as separate columns, and every price capture provenance-stamped.

SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
Origination order is fixed: side first, then the total, then the moneyline. The side is the deepest market and the one the points model is fitted against; the total comes from the same model and must not be tuned to the side's residual; the moneyline is derived from the joint side/total outcome distribution and is therefore originated last. Each market publishes three separate columns — fair line (our number), market line (the price-snapshot capture), and the difference (fair − market) — never combined into one cell.
