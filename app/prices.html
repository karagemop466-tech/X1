The schema a price feed must satisfy

  1. Open and close, both. CLV is measured against the close; origination value against the open.
  2. Timestamps. A price without a capture time cannot be audited after the fact (gate G-10).
  3. Per-selection rows. Sides and totals carry one number, moneylines two — keyed on (game, market, selection).
  4. Provenance per cell. Source URL, verification flag, and nothing filled from memory.
