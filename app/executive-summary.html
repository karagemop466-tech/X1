Executive summary

X1 exists to answer one question honestly: which NFL strategies are skill and which are a hot streak? Everything in this site is built to make that answer checkable rather than asserted.

1. The base layer is sound and independently verified.

All 32 standings rows, all 345 official injury rows and the Week 10 slate were re-fetched from NFL.com on 2026-11-14 and compared cell by cell against what this repository publishes: 2929 checks, 0 mismatches. Two independent transcriptions of the standings agree 32 of 32. The league's own arithmetic holds on every club: home plus road equals overall, points for minus points against equals net points.

2. The deficiency register now works, and it found what it is for.

Cross-referencing each club's printed depth chart with the league's injury report identifies 189 starting slots whose starter appears on the official report, of which 39 are ruled Out, 4 doubtful and 35 questionable — on both offence and defence. Rebuilding the join with a proper name normaliser corrected 42 cells; the most consequential is Cleveland's starting free safety Ronnie Hickman (Out, replaced by a 2nd-string) and three Green Bay starters whose jersey-numbered names never matched.

3. Our own number is not yet good enough to price against.

A walk-forward Poisson points model fitted only on earlier seasons produces a spread MAE of 10.98 points against the market benchmark's 9.73 over 2021–2026, across 7,154 scored games. Threshold sweeps return ROI between −6.1% and −2.6%. The honest conclusion is that the model is a screen, not a price, and it is published that way.

4. One of sixteen strategies is skill by our own pre-registered definition.

"Wind — under" (outdoor games with sustained wind, bet the under) returns +11.5% ROI over 398 out-of-sample bets from 2015 to 2026, hit rate 57.0%, bootstrap 95% interval +2.2% to +20.7%, sign-flip p = 0.008, and it stays positive when one-score games and blowouts are stripped out. Every other candidate fails at least one gate. That is the point of the gates: they are designed so that a lucky season cannot be crowned.

5. Two inputs block everything downstream.

Prices and results. Neither is published by the league or by any club, so the market-line table is empty and every outcome-dependent gate is currently unenforced rather than guessed. Six questions are hard blockers — they cannot be answered from any official source and they stop specific parts of the system from working: Q-01 bankroll, Q-02 price feed, Q-04 results feed, Q-05 paper or real money, Q-06 how rewards are defined, Q-11 player-prop source.

What to do next

The suggestion board lists twenty options, each with three paths, a cost, an effort, a risk and a recommendation, and each waiting for a decision rather than being built. Nothing on that board has been implemented without a recorded choice.
