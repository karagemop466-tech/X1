Signal vs staking, on the official-only board — schema locked before the data

The mirror-window grades above demonstrate the masking vector with real numbers. This is the schema the official-only board fills the day verified prices and graded results exist: a signal block (flat 1-unit grading: hit rate, mean CLV, CLV t-stat, edge at the close) and a staking block (ROI at rule, drawdown, growth) — and the promotion/demotion gates read the signal block first, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. Values are blank on purpose: ranks before graded data would be ranks of noise. Record: data/leaderboard_split.json.
