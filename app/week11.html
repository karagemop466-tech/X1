Note on the change flag

The 01:53 and 13:16 UTC reads are different bytes (the ledger flags the second as revised), but a diff of the two pages shows only analytics script tags and a carousel identifier changed. Their injury tables are identical, so the designation delta between them is none. The flag alone overstates change; the field-level delta above is the measure.

Charlie Heck (IND, T): a change in the official report, not a parse error

The 13:16 UTC read printed Not injury related - personal matter and Questionable for him. The live page, read at about 21:59 UTC on 2026-11-19, showed both cells blank, and the 22:10 UTC read captured on the runner shows the same. The change is therefore in the official report; the parser did not create it. The 22:10 UTC read prints no game designation for him. That is not a clearance and is not read as one: the report says nothing about his game status.
