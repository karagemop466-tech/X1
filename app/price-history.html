Conventions and limitations

Conventions (verbatim from each source)

  • closing_spread: signed game line; positive = home team favored (nflverse DATASETS.md)
  • closing_total: single game total
  • closing_ml: American odds for the named side
  • opening_spread: line is for the named team as published; negative = that team favored (initial_lines.csv)
  • opening_total: single game total; Over and Under published identical (verified)
  • opening_ml: American odds for the named side

Limitations

  • Opening and closing use different published conventions (per-team opening spread vs signed game closing spread). They are stored verbatim as published; normalising them into one convention is a follow-up before any CLV number is computed from this store.
  • 2021 is the only season with opening data, and it has no opening moneylines — CLV studies on moneylines are impossible from reachable sources (part of Q-02).
  • No value in this store is official. F-06 stands: a licensed or account price feed is the owner decision that would upgrade the tiers.
