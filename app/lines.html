Originated lines

Our own spread and total, how accurate it is, and the honest answer on whether it beats the market.

SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
What "originate our own lines" means here. A walk-forward Poisson points model is fitted only on seasons earlier than the one it scores, and produces its own spread and total for every game. It is then compared with the market benchmark so the size of the opportunity is measured rather than assumed. The benchmark is a third-party closing line carried in the nflverse/nfldata mirror and fingerprinted in data/raw_manifest.json; it is not a league publication, and every row that uses it says so.

Does our number beat the market? No — and that is the finding

Over the out-of-sample window the model's spread error is 10.98 points against the market benchmark's 9.73, and its total error is 11.17 points. Sweeping the bet threshold from 1 to 9 points of disagreement produces ROI between −6.1% and −2.6% on the spread — better as the threshold rises, but never positive.

So the model is published as a screen, not a price. It narrows the games worth looking at; it does not by itself produce an edge, and this page does not claim one.
