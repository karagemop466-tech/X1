SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
A live strategy that trips any trigger goes back to paper. The gate is pre-registered and armed before any strategy is live, so no result can be reclassified after the fact. Triggers are scored on flat stake where possible, so a lucky staking run cannot buy a failing signal another week.

What happens when a trigger fires

  1. strategy status set to paper (from live)
  2. stakes voided at next settle
  3. entry on the What changed page + flagged-for-review if disputed
  4. re-qualification: 4 weeks AND >= 30 paper signals at flat stake beating 0 ROI with CI covering nothing worse than -5%

Authority: pipeline gates job (data/pipeline/gates.json) — owner notified, veto via repository issue

Provenance of the thresholds

Thresholds are defaults pre-registered by this session per the owner instruction 'define a demotion gate that sends a live strategy back to paper'; numeric values are proposals on the suggestion board (S-20) and may be amended before any strategy goes live. Blank is never filled: triggers needing missing inputs (D-2) stay flagged until the input exists.
