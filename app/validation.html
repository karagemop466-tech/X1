Validation gates

Eight gates, pre-registered. Their whole job is to stop luck being paid as skill.

Only 'Wind — under' clears all eight. Note that the strategies with the largest nominal sample sizes are the ones that fail significance: more bets only helps if the edge is real.

Why the gates are built this way

  • Sample sufficiency (G1, ≥ 100 bets). Below that, a single lucky weekend moves ROI by more than any plausible edge.
  • Out-of-sample integrity (G2). Parameters are chosen from earlier seasons only; a bet may not appear twice.
  • Significance (G3). Bootstrap 95% interval lower bound above zero and a sign-flip randomisation p ≤ 0.05. The randomisation test replaced an earlier permutation test that was mathematically inert — permuting a sample cannot change its mean.
  • Economics (G4, ROI ≥ 2%). Significant but tiny is not investable after costs.
  • Baselines (G5). Must beat every always-on baseline on the same window.
  • Luck robustness (G6). Must stay positive once one-score games (margin ≤ 8) and blowouts (margin ≥ 25) are removed. Those two buckets are where variance, not skill, decides.
  • Parameter stability (G7). At least 60% of neighbouring parameter values must also be positive, so the result is not an artefact of one chosen cut-off.
  • Non-redundancy (G8, |r| ≤ 0.85). A strategy that is the same bet as an accepted one adds no information and earns no reward.

Any ONE trigger demotes. This gate is mechanical: triggers fire without discretion.
What demotion does: live -> paper, effective at the next settlement batch (immediately for D6). open live positions settle normally; new positions at paper stakes only. frozen: no additions or removals while demoted. status shows 'demoted' with the trigger id; the strategy cannot rank in live leaderboards. every demotion appended to data/demotion_log.jsonl (strategy, trigger, evidence, window, timestamp)
Re-entry (never automatic): 1. one full shadow window at paper stakes; 2. pass gates G0-G7 again on the shadow window; 3. one clean live-probation window before restored standing; 4. never automatic; re-entry is recorded in the demotion log; mechanical: triggers fire without discretion. Manual override requires an owner note in COORDINATION.md with reasons.
