The two that matter

F-01 and F-02 are the only rows where a published value is contradicted rather than merely unsupported. Both are quarterback names, both come from the same column, and both are the kind of detail that looks harmless and quietly poisons a model that trusts it. The fix is to stop publishing that column except where an NFL.com news story names the starter — four clubs this week instead of twenty-eight guesses.
