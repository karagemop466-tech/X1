Source register

Every page this project reads, what it is used for, and what the last run got back from it. "Official" here means published by the league or by a club on its own official site.

Nothing is published from a source that is not on this list. The allow-list is enforced in code, not by convention: scripts/verify_data.py fails if any row cites a host that is not nfl.com or a club domain the league itself lists.

Sources deliberately not used

  • Betting prices of any kind. No official source publishes them, and an unofficial price would be unverifiable by definition.
  • Third-party analytics sites. Convenient, but not official, and their numbers cannot be checked against a league page.
  • Anything recalled rather than read. If the value was not on the page at fetch time, it is not in the dataset.
