QUESTIONS · 2026-11-19

What has to be answered before a number can be trusted

Two registers, side by side and on purpose. The first is the research desk's own list of questions with the evidence already in hand and the measurement that would close each one. The second is the project register of open decisions, each with the information held and the specific evidence that would settle it. A question is answered here only when an official source settles it and that source is linked.

Read the third column before the first. Most of these questions are not unanswered because nobody looked; they are unanswered because the answer is not published anywhere, and inventing one is the only way to make the table look finished. The How to discover it column names the concrete next measurement.

The three that gate everything else

  1. Do we have the right to use the data we want?

    Play-by-play, snap counts and historical odds all sit behind terms this repository has not read. Until that is settled, only officially published aggregates are shown, and raw row-level data stays out of the public tree.

  2. Which price is the market?

    No official source publishes prices, and the four site trees in this repository name different benchmarks. A leaderboard that ranks across two benchmarks is not a leaderboard.

  3. What counts as a starter?

    The league publishes no depth chart. Every 2nd/3rd/4th-string claim depends on a club page that at least two clubs label “unofficial”. The mechanical definition used here — printed in column 1 of the club's own chart, stored as a group when a club lists several players in one slot — is auditable, but it is still a club self-report.
