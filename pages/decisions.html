How to read the Answer column

A blank answer means no verified answer exists. It does not mean nobody looked. Each row carries the information already held, which is often the reason we can say with confidence that the answer is still unknown — for example, knowing the league publishes no league-wide depth chart is what makes Q-05 a decision rather than a lookup.
