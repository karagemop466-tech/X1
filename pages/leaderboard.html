How ranking works

score = z_skill - lambda*calibration_penalty - mu*drawdown_penalty z_skill = (p̂ − p_break) / sqrt(p̂(1−p̂)/n_eff)

Rank by skill measured in units of its own uncertainty — never by raw ROI, which is a magnitude, not evidence.

Design rules that stop the board rewarding luck

  • Reward calibration independently of profit. A well-calibrated strategy that loses to margin is doing the hard part right.
  • No reward for a single hot window. Champion needs two consecutive qualifying windows.
  • Clawback. A title later shown to rest on a data error or rule violation is vacated, publicly.
  • No pay-to-enter. Charging entry while running the book side invites a regulatory classification nobody here can answer.
  • Fixed maximum entrants per window, declared before it opens.
