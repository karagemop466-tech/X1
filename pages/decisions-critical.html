The questions that block a decision

7 questions decide whether any strategy in this repository can honestly be scored. Each row states what is already known, what is missing, the evidence that would close the gap, and what becomes possible once it is closed. 3 are P0: nothing downstream is trustworthy until they are answered.

A question is answered only when an official source settles it and that source is linked. The P0/P1/P2 priority is about what unblocks the most work per unit of effort, not urgency.
