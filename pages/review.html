Immediate suggested path

  1. Agree the product's objective and the exact leaderboard competition rules.
  2. Acquire licensed odds history and verify official NFL outcome/availability access and time coverage.
  3. Build an immutable input/prediction ledger; leave role rank unknown unless an official dated source provides it.
  4. Run baseline and strategy candidates through all gates; report failures as well as passes.
  5. Start paper-only. Turn on rewards only after legal and product governance approval and prospective validation.
