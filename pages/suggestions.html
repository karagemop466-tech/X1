SUGGESTIONS

Suggestions register

18 reviewable proposals for improving the site and the decision process. Each carries its impact, its cost, what it depends on and a reversible first step — so the owner can pick the best path before any change is implemented.

Nothing on this page is implemented automatically. The suggestions are ranked by impact and effort only as a reading aid; the Decision column is the owner's to set. Two of them (S-01, S-02) are already visible in this release as patterns — the tier badges and the snapshot date in every header — and remain listed so the reviewer can accept or reject them explicitly.

S-01

Publish an evidence grade on every table row

impact: high effort: low depends on: none
Problem
A reader cannot tell a league-verdict value from a club self-report at a glance.
Proposal
Add a per-row badge: A = league-published fact, B = club-published, C = derived by our own documented join, U = unverified. Filter and sort by badge.
Why it improves decisions
Lets a reviewer apply a different trust threshold to A than to B without reading the methodology each time.
Reversible first step
Add the badge column to the generated tables and a legend on the Sources page.
Risk & mitigation
Badge inflation — someone marks a B as an A to close a flag. Mitigation: badge is set by the generator, not by hand.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-02

Add a 'staleness clock' to every page

impact: high effort: low depends on: none
Problem
Injury and schedule data decay within days, but a static page looks equally authoritative on day 1 and day 30.
Proposal
Render hours since retrieval next to each table; past 72 hours, show an amber banner; past 168 hours, show a red one saying the table must not be used for decisions.
Why it improves decisions
Prevents a decision being made on a snapshot the reader believes is live.
Reversible first step
Compute age in the build script from the retrieval timestamp already stored in each file header.
Risk & mitigation
No material risk identified.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-03

Extend depth-chart capture from 5 to all 32 clubs

impact: high effort: medium depends on: Q-14
Problem
Availability features cannot be compared across teams while 27 clubs are blank.
Proposal
Add one row per club to a capture list, with the club URL, a weekly timestamp, and the printed 'unofficial' label captured verbatim. Automate the fetch, diff against last week, and flag changes.
Why it improves decisions
Turns a 5-club anecdote into a league-wide dataset, which is the minimum for the starter-at-risk signal to be testable.
Reversible first step
Fill the capture list; record the four clubs that play Thursday first, since their reports lock earliest.
Risk & mitigation
Club pages change layout; a silent layout change could produce empty rows. Mitigation: the verifier fails when a chart yields zero players.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-04

Adopt a three-tier verification protocol in writing

impact: high effort: low depends on: none
Problem
Verification is currently a habit, not a contract.
Proposal
Tier 1: official league page (nfl.com / operations.nfl.com). Tier 2: official club page, explicitly labelled self-reported. Tier 3: independent secondary, for corroboration only. No Tier 3 value may populate a table; it may only appear in a flag's 'verified' column.
Why it improves decisions
Makes an argument about a number decidable by pointing at its tier.
Reversible first step
Publish the protocol on the Sources page and stamp every row with its tier.
Risk & mitigation
Tier 2 being treated as Tier 1 under deadline pressure. Mitigation: generator rejects a Tier 3 source URL in a data row.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-05

Build a pre-registration file per strategy before any test

impact: high effort: medium depends on: Q-12
Problem
Gates mean nothing if they are chosen after the results.
Proposal
One hash-sealed JSON per strategy: hypothesis, features, window, gates, staking, written before the window opens. Registering late is impossible because the hash is committed to git with a timestamp.
Why it improves decisions
Makes 'we would have known' auditable.
Reversible first step
Define the schema and commit one example pre-registration for a paper strategy.
Risk & mitigation
Overhead slows experimentation. Mitigation: template with sane defaults so a registration takes minutes.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-06

Report ROI with and without the top 5% of tickets

impact: high effort: low depends on: Q-02
Problem
A handful of long-odds winners can make a losing strategy look like a winning one.
Proposal
Show trimmed ROI beside raw ROI on every strategy row. Report the gap. Flag any strategy whose gap exceeds the margin.
Why it improves decisions
Distinguishes a strategy from a lottery ticket that happened to hit.
Reversible first step
Add the trimmed metric to the leaderboard schema now, so it cannot be bolted on after results exist.
Risk & mitigation
Trimming hides genuine big-edge bets. Mitigation: always show both; never show trimmed alone.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-07

Separate offense and defense availability indices

impact: medium effort: medium depends on: Q-04, S-03
Problem
A single 'team health' number averages unrelated things.
Proposal
Two indices per team, each with its own weights and its own gate. Never sum them into one score for ranking.
Why it improves decisions
A team can be healthy on offense and devastated on defense; the market treats those cases very differently.
Reversible first step
Define the two feature sets; keep them in separate files so they cannot be accidentally merged.
Risk & mitigation
Doubling the feature count doubles the multiple-comparison problem. Mitigation: correction already required by correlation.md.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-08

Add a scenario simulator for parlay pricing

impact: medium effort: medium depends on: Q-10
Problem
Parlay and combo pricing is where the arithmetic errors concentrate.
Proposal
A small simulator that takes per-leg probabilities and a correlation matrix, samples joint outcomes, and returns the fair parlay price with an interval. Show the naive independent product next to it.
Why it improves decisions
Makes the cost of assuming independence visible in one screen.
Reversible first step
Implement with synthetic parameters, clearly labelled as an illustration, not a market estimate.
Risk & mitigation
Synthetic output mistaken for a real edge. Mitigation: watermarked 'illustrative' and excluded from the leaderboard.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-09

Dedicate the Flagged page to a numbered, sortable review queue

impact: medium effort: low depends on: none
Problem
A long flag list with no ordering does not help anyone choose what to fix first.
Proposal
Every flag gets an id, a severity, a decision owner and a 'settles it' action. Sort by severity x blocking-ness. Show which open questions each flag unblocks.
Why it improves decisions
Converts a complaint list into a work queue with a defensible order.
Reversible first step
Cross-link each flag to the question ids it blocks (already recorded in the JSON).
Risk & mitigation
Queue grows faster than it is drained. Mitigation: show age per flag and a throughput number.
Decision

S-10

Run a second independent transcription pass on the current tables

impact: high effort: low depends on: none
Problem
Mechanical checks caught one error; the rest are only as good as one careful read.
Proposal
Have a second session or human re-read the standings and injury tables against the live official pages, row by row, and diff against the stored TSV. Any mismatch is a flag.
Why it improves decisions
The single highest-value action for trustworthiness, and it is cheap.
Reversible first step
Generate a blank transcription sheet from the TSV with the value column empty, for an independent filler.
Risk & mitigation
Both passes make the same systematic error (e.g. reading the wrong row). Mitigation: the second pass starts from the source page, not from our file.

S-11

Publish the market scope as a versioned contract

impact: medium effort: low depends on: Q-03, Q-09
Problem
'We do spreads and props' is not testable; the settlement of each market differs.
Proposal
A versioned file listing each in-scope market, its settlement rule, its data source and its gate profile. Changing the scope requires a new version number.
Why it improves decisions
Stops a mid-season scope change from silently invalidating a comparison.
Reversible first step
Draft v0.1 with spreads and totals only, and mark props as pending.
Risk & mitigation
Premature commitment. Mitigation: v0 explicitly excludes props with a stated reason.
Decision

S-14

Rank the leaderboard on signal only, keep staking in its own columns

impact: high effort: low depends on: n, o, t, h, i, n, g
Problem
A single ROI column lets a lucky staking run mask a bad signal; the board cannot tell the two apart.
Proposal
Adopt the split already published in data/leaderboard_signal_staking.json: rank strictly on signal_n / signal_z / signal_p against the registered baseline; publish ROI, units and drawdown in a separate staking group that is structurally excluded from rank.
Why it improves decisions
Rewards are then paid for measured skill only. A +40% ROI on 30 lucky bets cannot outrank a smaller but significant signal.
Reversible first step
Point the leaderboard rank at the signal group; keep staking columns visible but unranked.
Risk & mitigation
Headline ROIs on the public board get smaller — that is the honest number.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically

S-16

Ratify the G8 demotion thresholds before anything goes live

impact: high effort: low depends on: Q, -, 2, 1
Problem
The demotion gate exists with four proposed trigger numbers, but proposals ratified after results exist are worthless (Q-21).
Proposal
Owner accepts or edits the four numbers in data/gates.json G8 and records the ratification date; no strategy may be promoted to live until that date exists.
Why it improves decisions
Fixes the exit before entering, so demotion cannot be negotiated away by a strategy that is currently lucky.
Reversible first step
Edit four thresholds, stamp the date, publish on the validation page.
Risk & mitigation
Thresholds set too tight cause churn; too loose and decay persists. The rolling windows (100 / 50 bets) bound both errors.
Decision
proposed — set by the owner; nothing here is implemented automatically
S-17

Automate the weekly results feed refresh

impact: high effort: medium depends on: Q, -, 2, 4
Problem
This round's 137 results were read page by page; without a scheduled step the feed silently goes stale and every backtest on it becomes wrong (Q-24).
Proposal
Add a by-week results step to data-refresh.yml: re-read completed weeks from nfl.com, re-run the three checks in tools/build_results.py, fail the build on any mismatch.
Why it improves decisions
Keeps the foundation of every ROI number fresh and self-checking; a layout change on nfl.com breaks the build loudly instead of corrupting data quietly.
Reversible first step

Risk & mitigation
