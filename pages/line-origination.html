Line origination

How to make our own numbers rather than copy the market — and why this page contains no prices.

Why no prices here: a price is only published after a registered model version runs on a frozen, licensed input snapshot and passes every validation gate. Anything else is a guess with a decimal point.

The three numbers you must never confuse

NumberWhat it isWhere it comes from
<b>Fair line</b>Our probability converted with zero margin, <code>1/p</code>Our model
<b>Market line</b>A book's price, which contains marginA licensed, timestamped quote
<b>Executable line</b>What we could actually get, at a stated book, limit and timeSame quote plus limits and rules

The pipeline (every strategy, no exceptions)

freeze inputs (as-of timestamp)
   → estimate a full outcome distribution
   → convert to fair odds (zero margin)
   → compare with a market quote of the SAME market, side and rules
   → log the forecast BEFORE kickoff (including passes)
   → grade after settlement
   → score only inside the registered test window

A failure at any arrow means the strategy is not scored. Unscored is not the same as losing: it is unknown, and unknown never enters the leaderboard.

Probability → price

fair decimal odds = 1 / p fair American = −100·p/(1−p) if p > 0.5, else +100·(1−p)/p implied probability of a quote = 1 / decimal_odds  (always includes margin)

A spread or total is not a single probability. It needs a distribution: P(home wins by ≥ k) and P(push) come from a modelled margin distribution, not a point estimate. Without a variance term you cannot price a 3.5 against a 3, and that difference is exactly where thin edges live.

Availability signal, defined precisely

avail_i        ∈ {full, limited, dnp}
designation_i  ∈ {-, questionable, doubtful, out}
depth_rank_i   = rank on the club's published chart   (1 = column 1)
snap_share_i   = trailing 4-game snap share           (licensed data needed)

starter_at_risk = (depth_rank_i == 1) AND (designation_i != "-")
replacement     = the player printed directly behind i on the same chart line
deficit_T       = Σ_i w_i · 1[starter_at_risk_i]       (w_i from snap share, never from opinion)

replacement is printed, not guessed. That is why depth charts are stored verbatim: a reviewer can check whether “next man up” is a real claim or an invention. Because w_i needs licensed snap data, the deficit number cannot be computed today, and publishing a hand-weighted version would be fabrication wearing a formula.

Player props are a different problem

  1. Opportunity model — routes run, targets, carries, red-zone snaps. Opportunity precedes production.
  2. Role model — probability the player holds the role the line assumes. Depth chart and designation drive this.
  3. Efficiency model — yards per route or per carry, adjusted for opponent.
  4. Distribution — count stats are over-dispersed; use a negative-binomial-style model, never a Poisson.
  5. Push handling — a whole-number line can push, so it has three outcomes and is not a coin flip.
