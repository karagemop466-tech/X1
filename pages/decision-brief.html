3 · What you have to decide, in dependency order

  1. 1. Do we operate on official public pages only, or fund a licensed data agreement?

    It decides whether the price, snap-count and play-by-play work can exist at all.

  2. 2. Which price source is the benchmark, and which number is &lsquo;the market&rsquo;?

    Four site trees currently name different benchmarks; a leaderboard cannot rank across them.

  3. 3. Does a club's self-labelled &lsquo;unofficial&rsquo; chart count as evidence?

    It is the only depth source that exists, and it is what every replacement-depth number rests on.

  4. 4. What exactly sends a live strategy back to paper?

    The demotion gate is written but its thresholds are not ratified, so nothing can be promoted safely.

  5. 5. What funds the rewards, and who may enter?

    Everything about the competition's integrity depends on this, including whether NFL personnel may play.

4 · The honest position on origination

  • The model is a screen, not a price. On verified 2026 results nothing beats break-even at −110. That is published, not hidden.
  • No official source publishes betting prices, so every market, closing-line and ROI cell is blank and flagged rather than filled from a mirror without a label.
  • Nothing is ranked on luck. The leaderboard separates signal from staking, and the demotion gate sends a strategy back to paper on measured decay rather than on a bad weekend.
  • Parlays and prop combos stay behind a joint-evidence gate. Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence; a combo is ranked only when a directly validated joint probability exists.
