The decision this round needs

Publish the 31-club depth join, or state that five clubs are all the evidence you want? The site currently reports 6 starters ruled out on the strength of a five-club pilot. The same join across the 31 clubs already collected returns 30. Nothing about the published number is false; it is a floor that reads like a total. Recommendation, cost and reversal are on the suggestions board (B-01); the question is Q-19.

The four steps recommended before anything else

  1. Publish the 31-club depth join instead of the five-club pilot — Turns a floor into a real count (5 clubs -> 31 clubs), which is the difference between 'at least 8 starters out' and the actual number.
  2. Make the independent re-transcription a required CI gate — The existing checks compare two transcriptions by our own sessions; this one catches a bad read even when both agree.
  3. One review queue, generated from every register — Four different counts of 'flagged items' is the single biggest obstacle to deciding what to fix first.
  4. Pick one line benchmark and stamp it on every result — Without it, the leaderboard ranks strategies measured against different rulers.

What is still not known

54 items remain in the review queue. The largest are the missing line benchmark (Q-02), the missing starter definition (Q-05), the absent licensed odds feed, and the fact that the only strategy passing all eight gates is measured against a third-party mirror rather than a league feed. None of them blocks publishing; all of them block acting on a result.
