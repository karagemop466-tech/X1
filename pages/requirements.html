Minimum questions to close before a first leaderboard run

  1. Define the forecast target, markets, grading rules, and decision time.
  2. Obtain permissioned historical outcomes and market prices with timestamped snapshots.
  3. Set the source-of-truth hierarchy for player status, roles, stats, and corrections.
  4. Choose baselines, risk metrics, holdout period, and acceptance gates before modeling.
  5. Publish leaderboard eligibility and reward terms only after legal and operational review.
