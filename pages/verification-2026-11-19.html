This session's own live reads, against what the site already published

On 2026-11-19 this session re-read four official nfl.com pages and compared every cell with the committed 2026-11-19 pack. Three datasets agree exactly. The fourth — the Week 11 injury report — agrees on 252 of 253 player rows and disagrees on one game-status cell, and its free-text injury column changed between consecutive reads of the same page. Both are published here rather than resolved.

Method. Each read is transcribed into its own dated file with the source URL on every row, then diffed cell by cell by a tool that runs in CI. A difference is a finding, never an overwrite: nothing in the committed pack was edited by this session, and no value was inferred to make a comparison close. Where the two reads disagree, the cell is published as unverified and appears on the Flagged for review page.

6 · What this session could not verify

  • nfl.com was reachable through the page-fetch tool only. A direct curl from this sandbox fails at TLS (exit 35), so no raw bytes were retained and no payload hash is claimed. Only the parsed text was read.
  • Three of seven injury chunks were read twice (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, plus a repeat of 0). Chunks 5–6 are navigation and the news carousel; the club blocks end in chunk 4.
  • No price, closing line or market figure is verified anywhere in this repository, and this page does not add one. See line origination for why the columns are blank.
