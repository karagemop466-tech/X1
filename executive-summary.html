Executive Summary

A concise, decision-oriented overview of the X1 NFL decision & signal system — what it does, what is verified and live today, what is pending, and the decisions required before scaling.

Purpose

X1 is an autonomous system to collect, organize, and analyze NFL data in order to originate our own betting lines. It treats line generation as a leaderboard competition in which strategies earn standing only after passing validation gates that remove luck and isolate skill. Defensive and offensive deficiency tracking (starters replaced by 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups) is a core edge signal. The site is the review surface: every figure is traced to an official source, and unverified values are left blank and flagged rather than guessed.

Decisions required (see Open Questions & Suggestions)

  1. Data feed — which official/authoritative feeds to standardize on for play-by-play, EPA, and betting lines (proposed in Open Questions).
  2. Validation thresholds — set minimum sample size, out-of-sample windows, and statistical-significance bars before any strategy is admitted (Validation Gates).
  3. Correlation policy — decide how correlation is measured and applied to parlay probability before building parlay strategies (Correlations).
  4. Leaderboard rewards — define ranking metric and reward tiers, gated by validation (Leaderboard).
Bottom line. The verification backbone and data pipeline are live and proven against official sources. The analytical engine (own lines, strategy performance, correlations) is specified and gated but intentionally left blank until real data is computed — no fabricated numbers are published. The Suggestions board is the review surface for choosing the next implementation path.
