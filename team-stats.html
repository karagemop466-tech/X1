Notes on these tables

  • Values are published exactly as printed by the official page. Nothing is recalculated, rounded or derived.
  • Column names come from the page's own header row. Every table on this page reports whether that header was present; where it was not, the row is flagged rather than being given a plausible label.
  • These are season totals to date, not per-game rates, so they favour teams that have played more games. The standings table shows games played per club.
  • Offensive "receiving" is the ball-catcher view of the passing game, so it correlates almost perfectly with passing output. That is a property of the data, not an error.

These tables are the input to the correlation values and to the side and total models described on the lines page.
