Suggestions — Review Board

Proposed improvements to the site, data, and decision-making. Each suggestion is listed with its rationale and impact so a decision-maker can review and choose the best path before any change is implemented. Status reflects the current decision state.

How to use this board. Suggestions are proposals, not commitments. Review each item, compare impact vs. effort, and mark a decision (Approve, Defer, or Reject) before implementation. Approving a suggestion creates the follow-up task tracked in COORDINATION.md. Nothing here is implemented until a decision is recorded.

S-01 · Source-link every data rowImplemented

Attach an official-source link to every numeric row on every data page, and leave blank plus flag anything that cannot be confirmed.

Rationale: directly enforces the no-hallucination requirement and makes every figure auditable. Impact: high trust, low effort. Evidence: standings, injuries, and lines pages now carry per-row official links (Sources).
