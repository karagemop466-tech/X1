Executive summary

Six new mechanisms are built and running: a price-snapshot store, a verified results feed, an originated line (side, then total, then moneyline), a leaderboard that scores signal and staking separately, a replacement-depth model, and correlations that carry their own n. None of them produced a bettable edge, and the summary says why in numbers rather than adjectives.

The results feed is complete and internally consistent (7,413 scored games, 0 arithmetic mismatches). The price store is structurally incomplete: 20,336 market rows, 0 of which have both an opening and a closing number, because the two files that evidence them cover different seasons and neither names a book or a timestamp. The model originates every market but loses to the market on every metric (side MAE 10.741 vs 9.792, Brier 0.241 vs 0.212). The leaderboard has 0 live strategies. One strategy shows a positive ROI and it is the one with the weakest signal -- the exact case the signal/staking split exists to catch -- and the demotion gate sends it back to paper on drawdown and on price loss.

If you read one thing

The model does not beat the market. It beats a naive constant, which means it carries real information, and it loses to the market on MAE and on calibration, which means that information is not an edge. No stake is being placed, and no strategy is live. The value delivered this round is that the system can now tell the difference — it caught a parlay showing +10.5% ROI and demoted it because its signal was indistinguishable from chance.

What would change the answer

  • S-L1 path B: capture one named book with a timestamp, twice, and publish the check.
  • S-L3 path A: build the two-season injury-to-line coefficient, and let it fail loudly if it fails.
  • S-L4 path B: a named price tier with two-source agreement, so T14's market column becomes a market someone can bet into.
  • S-L2 path A: rewards as rank and scrip, so requirement R-10 is satisfied without a legal exposure this repository cannot assess.
