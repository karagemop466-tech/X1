The control gate on every leaderboard run

A strategy beats the controls only if its win rate is above both controls on the same games, and its 95% lower bound is above the 52.4% bar. The gate is written before any result is seen and cannot be applied to a window with no price.

What the controls show, and what they do not

  • Random picks centre on 50.0%. They sit inside a band of 47.8%–52.1%. A pick that does nothing scores about 50%, before any price.
  • Favourites win 48.2% against the spread. That is below the 52.4% bar at −110. 94.3% of random runs score at least as well as favourites.
  • Straight up, favourites win 66.4%. That number is not a betting edge, because the price is the thing being bet against.
  • Not shown: any strategy result on 2026. A leaderboard win rate of 50–52% is not evidence of skill until it is measured against these controls on the same games.
