What is withheld, and why it stays withheld. market_line and residual are empty on all 52 rows and CLV and ROI are not computed at all. This repository has no licensed price source for 2026 — open question Q-02, and the pinned third-party mirror prints a spread and a total for these fixtures and prints no score for any of them, which is measured on Feed closure. The fair line is published because the model produced it before kickoff; the residual is the number an origination programme is actually judged on, and publishing it from data the owner has not agreed to use would rank a strategy on a source nobody has cleared. When a price policy is decided the residual fills retroactively, because the fair line was recorded first — that is the entire purpose of an escrow.

Limits, from the artefact, not softened: margin model: no opponent adjustment, personnel, venue, rest or weather inside the rating; total_A is the league average by construction; publishing it is the floor total_B has to beat, not an estimate of any particular game; the Mexico City fixture (Vikings at 49ers) receives the frozen 1.6-point home edge although the official fixture page prints Location MEXICO CITY and Stadium ESTADIO BANORTE; the rule is frozen for this window, so the edge is published as the rule states it and the venue is recorded as a limit, for the next registration to decide on; rest_days columns are published context derived from official schedule dates; the model does not use them in this registration; the settled aggregate has n = 1 and is a demonstration of the mechanism, not a result.
