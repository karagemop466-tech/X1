A backtest can be computed at all now. The feed carries the final score, the derived margin and total, a settlement rule with an explicit push clause, and the file hash of the exact bytes the scores came from. That is the minimum for a result to count.

The settlement rule, written down once

MarketRulePush handlingArtefact
Sidehome_covers = (home_margin + home_line) > 0. A line of -3 means the home team must win by more than 3.home_margin + home_line == 0 is a PUSH; stake returned, not counted as a win or a loss, and excluded from the hit-rate denominator.settlement_rule
Totalover_wins = (home_score + away_score) > total_line.exact equality is a PUSH and is excluded from the hit rate.settlement_rule
Moneylinethe named side wins outright; a tie is a PUSH on the moneyline and is excluded (NFL regular-season ties are possible).a tie is a push and is excludedsettlement_rule
ROIsum(profit) / sum(stake), stake = 1 unit per bet unless a staking rule is registered separately. A win at American price p returns decimal d (d = 1 + p/100 for p >= +100, d = 1 + 100/|p| otherwise), so profit = d - 1; a loss = -1; a push = 0.—settlement_rule

Published once so the leaderboard, the backtest and the gates cannot disagree about what a win means.

Void rules: A game with no published final score is not graded. A game with no published price is graded at the line (push-aware) and counted in the `no_price` bucket, never silently priced at -110. A neutral-site game is flagged, not dropped.
