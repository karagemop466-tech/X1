What cannot be decided yet, and why that is fine

Every question above has a closing test that is a number, not an opinion: two captures of the same book that agree, a coefficient whose interval excludes zero, a licence that permits publication. That is deliberate. A decision made before its question closes is a guess with a paper trail, and the paper trail is the only part that helps later.
