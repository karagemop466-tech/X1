When a strategy goes back to paper

Four automatic triggers, evaluated every build, that demote a live strategy.

SNAPSHOT 2026-11-14 · WEEK 10
A gate that cannot fire is not a gate. All four triggers are evaluated on every build, and the verdict is published here even when it demotes a row with a positive ROI. On this snapshot it does exactly that.

Why the parlay is demoted even though it made money

It shows a +10.5% ROI at a flat stake, which on a naive board would rank it first. Its signal z is 1.00 against a 1.96 bar — indistinguishable from chance — and under a 5%-of-bankroll compounding plan its drawdown reaches 52.7%, more than twice the 25% cap. D-03 also fires: the mean CLV proxy over its last 30 tickets is negative. A strategy that cannot survive a change of stake plan and is taking worse numbers than the close is a staking result, not a signal, and it goes back to paper.
