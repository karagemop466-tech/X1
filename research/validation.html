Validation gates

A strategy earns reward only by passing all eight gates. The gates are applied identically to every strategy, and every failure is published with the measured value so the decision to reject or fix is transparent.

Gate definitions

GateNameThresholdType
G1Sample sufficiencyAt least 100 settled out-of-sample betsGate
G2Out-of-sample integrityParameters chosen only from earlier seasons; no duplicate betsGate
G3Statistical significanceBootstrap 95% CI lower bound above zero and permutation p <= 0.05Gate
G4Economic significanceROI of at least 2% per unit stakedGate
G5Beats naive baselinesROI above every always-on baseline on the same windowGate
G6Luck robustnessPositive ROI after removing one-score games and blowoutsGate
G7Parameter stabilityAt least 60% of neighbouring parameter values stay positiveGate
G8Non-redundancyCorrelation with any accepted strategy at most 0.85Gate
