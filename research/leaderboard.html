Leaderboard

The competition table. Reward is paid only to strategies that clear all eight validation gates; a rejected strategy earns zero and is shown with the gates it failed, so the board shows the evidence rather than just the winners.

Reward formula

reward = 1000 × ROI × min(1, bets / 300) × confidence

confidence is the share of the bootstrap confidence interval that stays above zero, so a strategy that is profitable but statistically fragile earns proportionally less. A strategy that fails any gate earns zero by definition.
