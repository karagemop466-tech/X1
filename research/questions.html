Q1Line modelWhich team strength inputs should the originated line use (Elo only, or a full drive-based efficiency model)?A walk-forward Elo and a walk-forward Poisson points model are both implemented; the Poisson model reproduces the market spread to 10.4 points MAE against the market's 9.7 (2021-2026 window).Whether drive-level EPA data would close the remaining gap to the market; play-by-play for the full history was not reachable from the sandbox.Add a play-by-play source (GSIS feed or an nflverse mirror) and refit with EPA-based efficiency; re-run the gate suite and compare out-of-sample MAE.HighOpen
Q2Line modelShould the originated line be published as a standalone number or only as a difference against the market line?Both are produced; the difference (model spread minus market spread) is what the strategies consume.How the line will actually be used operationally - as a pricing input, a screen, or a published show line.Decision needed from the business owner; this determines whether calibration to the market is allowed.HighOpen
Q3ValidationWhat minimum sample should a strategy need before it earns a reward?The current gate floor is 100 settled out-of-sample bets (G1).The risk appetite behind the floor, and whether 100 bets is the right number for a strategy whose edge is 11% ROI.Agree the floor with the decision owner; the gate threshold is a single constant in src/x1/config.py.MediumOpen
Q4ValidationShould the reward pool be flat, ROI-based, or risk-adjusted?Reward = 1000 x ROI x min(1, bets/300) x confidence, where confidence is the share of the bootstrap interval that stays above zero.Whether the reward should reward profit, calibration, or data quality.Decision needed; the formula is isolated in src/x1/leaderboard.py.MediumOpen
Q5Market dataWhich consensus line should be treated as 'the market' for both validation and the leaderboard?The project uses the closing lines carried in the nfldata mirror (7,439 games with a line).Whether the intended benchmark is a single sportsbook, a consensus of books, or a market-weighted average; also whether opening lines should be stored for closing-line-value measurement.Specify the benchmark, then re-run; a per-book file (2013-2020) is already in the repository for comparison.HighOpen
Q6InjuriesWhich injury feed becomes the system of record for weekly status?The league publishes the official injury report each week; the current-week report was transcribed directly from nfl.com (107 rows).Whether an automated pull from the league page can be relied on for every week, and how quickly the system must refresh (the league files practice reports at 4 p.m. ET on Wednesday-Friday).Confirm the operating environment (network access, schedule) and implement the automated pull with a checksum per week.HighOpen
Q7InjuriesWhere does an authoritative depth chart come from?No league-operated source publishes depth tiers. The project derives tiers from 2026 usage (pass attempts, targets, carries) and games played.Whether a maintained depth-chart feed is acceptable, and whether 7 weeks of usage is a stable enough basis for tiering.Compare derived tiers against a maintained feed for one full season; measure how often the derived tier-1 differs from the published first string.HighOpen
Q8InjuriesHow much should a missing starter be worth in the line?Measured 2026 effect of an Out player at each position group: teams missing an Out linebacker still covered the spread in 76.7% of 43 games (average +7.1 points); the sample is small and one season.The stable, multi-season value of each injury, and whether it interacts with the market line.Extend the measurement across multiple seasons once a multi-year injury feed is available; fit a regression of ATS margin on injury context.MediumOpen
Q9Player propsWhich player-prop markets should be targeted, and what is the priced market line?Originated prop lines are built for passing yards, rushing yards, receiving yards and receptions, with calibrated hit rates of 50.3%, 48.1%, 48.8% and 42.8% on the 2021+ evaluation window.The market's prop lines and prices, so no edge can be measured and no prop strategy can be validated.Acquire a prop-line feed; until then prop strategies are published as research, not as leaderboard entries.HighOpen
Q10Player propsWhich prop combinations are worth building given correlation?Game-level correlations: QB passing yards vs receiver receiving yards +0.93 (near-duplicate), receiving yards vs receptions +0.77, RB rushing vs receiving yards -0.16.How the sportsbook prices correlated parlays, and whether a same-game or cross-game combination is better once prices are known.Once prop prices exist, re-run the break-even table in src/x1/correlation.py with the real leg prices.MediumOpen
Q11ParlaysAre parlays ever worth building at -110 legs?Two -110 legs need a 27.5% joint hit rate (a 52.4% leg hit rate if independent). Both parlay strategies built here landed between 21.4% and 25.8% joint hit rates and lost money.Whether a leg with a real price advantage (not -110) changes the arithmetic in practice.Re-test the parlay builders with price-aware selection once a priced feed with per-leg odds is available.MediumOpen
Q12ValidationHow should luck be removed beyond the one-score and blowout filters?Luck gate removes games decided by 8 points or fewer and by 25 points or more; the surviving strategy improves from +11.5% to +13.0% ROI.Turnover luck, special-teams luck and drive-level variance, which need play-by-play data.Add drive-level data, then compute a luck decomposition (variance share attributable to turnovers, one-score outcomes and garbage-time scoring).MediumOpen
Q13OperationsHow often should the pipeline run, and how are new weeks ingested?The pipeline is idempotent and re-runnable; the raw manifest records a SHA-256 for every input file.The production schedule and who watches for a failed week.Schedule the GitHub Actions workflow weekly (Tuesday through Friday, before the Friday 4 p.m. ET game-status deadline) and add an alerting path.HighOpen
Q14GovernanceWhat is the unit of competition for the leaderboard - per strategy, per strategy family, or per analyst?The leaderboard currently ranks strategies and pays a reward per strategy.Whether the competition is between strategies or between people submitting strategies.Decision needed; it changes how the reward table is attributed.LowOpen
Q15BankrollWhat staking plan should the reward be converted into?All results are expressed per unit staked at the published price (-115 where the source has no price).Whether the real book price differs materially from the mirror's price, and whether flat staking or Kelly is intended.Capture live prices for the next 100 bets and compare realised price with the mirrored price; then decide the staking rule.MediumOpen
