Suggestions for review

Nothing here has been implemented on the strength of this page alone. Each suggestion states the problem, the proposed change, the evidence that justifies it, and the impact, effort, dependencies and risk, so a path can be chosen deliberately. The status column is the review decision point: Proposed becomes Accepted, Deferred or Rejected.

SG2InjuriesBuy or license a real depth-chart feedDepth tiers are the weakest link in the injury tracker: usage-derived tiers work for quarterbacks, running backs, receivers and tight ends, but defensive groups cannot be ranked from offensive usage.Contract a maintained depth-chart provider, or license the underlying club-published charts, and re-derive the deficiency table from them.For the nine teams in the current report, 45 of 72 team/position-group combinations could be ranked from usage; all 27 unranked combinations are defensive groups (plus four offensive lines) where no player leads outright on games played.HighMediumBudget approvalCost; the derived approach still covers the offensive groups.Proposed
SG3Line modelAdd drive-level play-by-play to the feature setThe model works from scores, rest, weather and Elo. Drive efficiency (EPA, success rate, early-down efficiency, third-down rate) is the standard missing input.Ingest a play-by-play source, add EPA-based offence and defence ratings with opponent adjustment, and refit the points model.The current model is 10.42 points MAE on the spread against the market's 9.73 (2021-2026).HighHighPlay-by-play access (SG10)More features can overfit; the walk-forward gate suite catches this, but keep the feature count disciplined.Proposed

SG5ValidationStore opening lines to measure closing-line valueThere is no way to tell whether a bet was made at a good price, because only the closing line is stored for recent seasons.Persist opening and closing lines per game and add a CLV column to every bet record and to the leaderboard.The repository has per-book lines for 2013-2020 (sc_lines.csv) and a closing-line file for 2006-2018; 2019+ is single-line only.HighMediumA source that publishes both opening and closing pricesAvailability of historical opening lines for older seasons.Proposed
SG6OperationsAdd a betting-campaign tracker for the leaderboardThe leaderboard measures historical performance; there is no forward record of what the system actually recommended and what happened.Emit a weekly recommendation file (game, side, line, model line, edge, gate status) and settle it automatically once results are published.The current pipeline can produce the list; the settling step and the archive do not exist yet.HighMediumQ13 schedule decisionLow.Proposed
SG7Line modelTreat wind as a first-class model input, not a strategy filterWind is the only signal in the library that survives every gate, and it is currently a post-hoc filter on the market total.Add wind speed and roof status to the points model, then re-test whether the wind effect survives as a line adjustment rather than a bet.Outdoors games with wind of 12 mph or more went over in 44.3% of 1,231 games (2006-2026) and the effect is present in all four five-year eras.HighLowWeather data with better coverage than the mirrorThe market may begin pricing wind faster; re-run the gate suite each season.Proposed
SG8ValidationRetire the strategies that only lose to the vigNine strategies sit between -2% and -9% ROI, which is simply the book margin. They crowd the leaderboard and consume review time.Keep them in the repository as negative controls (they prove the gate suite rejects noise) but publish them collapsed under a 'rejected' section rather than as live candidates.The naive baselines are -2.70% (always favourite), -31.85% (always underdog), -1.87% (always over) and +1.87% (always under) on the out-of-sample window.MediumLowNo dependencyHiding a strategy that might improve with a better model; mitigate by re-running the suite after every model change.Proposed
SG9Player propsBuild the prop line model on drive efficiency, not rolling meansThe prop model is a league-relative rolling mean; it is calibrated (hit rates within 2 points of 50% on the evaluation window) but it cannot price a prop against a market line.Rebuild the projection on role-adjusted opportunity shares (targets, carries, red-zone touches) and add opponent defensive coverage adjustment.Current dispersion: 88.3 yards (QB passing), 30.2 (RB rushing), 30.9 (receiving), 2.0 (receptions).MediumHighProp price feed (Q9)Without market lines the improvement cannot be measured in ROI terms.Proposed
SG10DataStand up a mirrored play-by-play archiveBulk data arrives through a community mirror; the sandbox allow-list blocks the release assets, so the richest datasets are unreachable directly.Mirror the needed releases into the repository's own data store with per-file SHA-256 checksums in the manifest, and document the refresh job.The raw manifest records 17 files with checksums; the mirror updates roughly every 20 minutes.MediumLowNo dependencyRepository size; keep large files outside git and fetch by checksum.Proposed
SG11DataAdd weather as an owned datasetWeather arrives through the game table, so it is missing whenever the schedule has not been published and it cannot be forecast for future games.Keep a stadium-level weather table (stadium, roof, orientation) and join a forecast feed for upcoming games.The current temperature field is present for 5,023 of 5,196 outdoor games (96.7%) and absent for every roofed game.MediumMediumStadium master dataLow.Proposed
SG12ReportingPublish a weekly human-readable briefThe site is comprehensive but dense; a decision maker wants a one-page brief each week.Generate a weekly brief (top signals, current injuries that matter, model lines versus market lines, and what changed since last week) from the same payload that feeds the site.All of the material already exists in the site payload.MediumLowSG6 for the forward recordLow.Proposed
SG13ValidationLock the evaluation protocol before the next seasonGate thresholds and the reward formula were chosen while building the system; changing them after seeing results would be p-hacking.Freeze the eight gates, the thresholds and the reward formula in a versioned protocol file, and require a new version to change them.The thresholds live in src/x1/config.py today; the survival of one strategy (+11.53% ROI, all gates passed) was measured under exactly this protocol.HighLowQ3 and Q4 decisionsLow.Proposed

How to review

Work down the Impact column. SG13 (lock the evaluation protocol) should be decided before any strategy is re-scored, and SG3, SG7 and SG9 change the model itself, so they should be decided before the next season starts. SG6 (campaign tracker) is what turns this from a research repository into something whose forward record can be audited.
