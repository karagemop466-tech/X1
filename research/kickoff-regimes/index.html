Flagged for review · what would change the decision?

Unknown: recovery probability, change in total points and change in comeback probability. The frozen PR-04 total B field named our_probability carries a difference in points, not a probability. KR-T1 does not use it. Final scores cannot identify a kick, the pre-kick score or its outcome. No replacement probability, fixed points penalty or strategy reward is published.

  1. Can we measure how often a team declares? First verify a rights-cleared official play-level source with all opportunities, including ordinary kicks. A log of successful recoveries alone gives the wrong denominator.
  2. Does the filter improve predictions? Freeze the rule, benchmark, window and trial count before the next evaluation. Compare otherwise identical models on the same chronological holdout; report game-cluster uncertainty and sample size.
  3. Retain a new feature only if prospective improvement clears the registered gate. If the gain disappears or evidence is incomplete, keep the existing baseline and the candidate in paper testing.

Source for rule scope: 2026 NFL Rulebook, Rule 6. The research gates are project policy, not NFL findings.
