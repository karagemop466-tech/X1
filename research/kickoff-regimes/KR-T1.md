# KR-T1 · Week 11 regulation-kickoff regime / full-game totals experiment

Paper only. Registration = first Git commit containing this file. No Week 11 final, market line, injury level, actual kickoff event or staking result is an input. This tests a same-regime team-context distribution, **not** the causal effect of a rule change. One candidate, one benchmark, no tuning.

Evaluation: every 2026 regular-season Week 11 game, including overtime in the final total. The threshold is PR-04’s already originated total A for the fixture, not a bettable price. One vector per game: probability of the integer final total being under / equal / over that threshold. Never interpret PR-04’s total B gap (points) as a probability. Source slate: https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-11 . Rule: https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/2026-nfl-rulebook .

Input pins (SHA-256): 150 verified finals in `data/results/verified_results_2026_r3.csv` = `d807853fff21a687f7bb270defc62a2fd3fe9d9b99a69b30c0b197ac92311161`; PR-04 escrow `data/lab/escrow/week11_forecasts_2026-11-17.csv` = `9050d41f36de779ef26cf3633235537b19b6bde7f594de70c377a9cf86ec453f`. Exclude other seasons, future reads, the entire cutoff date (2026-11-19 UTC), and week 11 onward. Publish evidence before the earliest kickoff or void the entire experiment. No rolling refresh; a new window needs a new registration.

Benchmark: count the eligible league totals in each outcome class. Candidate: count the same outcomes with weight 2 for a game involving either participating team and weight 1 otherwise (an earlier head-to-head counts only once at weight 2). Use the feed's team abbreviations; normalize ARZ/ARI to AZ and LAR to LA. Add 0.5 to each feasible class, normalize over the feasible classes. A half-point threshold makes a push impossible. No other fitted parameters. Publish raw and weighted counts, timestamp, hashes, probabilities and official fixture URL. No market residual, CLV, odds, ROI, correlation or ranking inferred.

Verification and decision: score only when all 13 final scores have official Game Centre evidence. Score = sum over the three classes of (p − indicator)^2, averaged across fixtures, comparing both distributions on identical games. Report paired improvement (benchmark minus candidate loss), n, and a paired game bootstrap 95% percentile interval (10,000 draws, seed 20261119). A positive lower bound warrants only a new preregistered, multi-week replication; an upper bound at or below zero retires the candidate. Otherwise inconclusive. Never award a leaderboard place, stake or cash prize from this pilot, regardless of result.
