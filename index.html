EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Five findings that change the plan

  1. No league-wide official depth chart exists. Clubs publish their own; Detroit and Green Bay literally print “unofficial”. So “2nd-string replacement” is a club self-report, not a league fact.
  2. “Starter” is a definition, not a lookup. We define it mechanically — printed in column 1 — so the definition can be re-run and audited.
  3. 55% is not skill. At −110 the break-even is 52.38%; 55.1% over 500 bets is z≈1.22.
  4. Parlay margin compounds. At −110 one leg carries a 4.55% hold; five legs carry 20.75% (≈4.6×). And assuming independence across two dependent 50% legs with ρ=0.30 misprices a +264 ticket by +18.5% in the bettor's favour.
  5. Verification caught our own error. Arizona was typed 2–6; the official page prints 3–6. The gate rejected it and the correction is public (F-08).
