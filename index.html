Executive summary

What is ready—and what is not

A reviewable plan has been built; no model has passed a performance test.

Reviewed 14 Nov 2026

Recommendation

Start with a source-audited, pregame forecast ledger and a paper-only leaderboard. Add predictions and odds only after the data license, timestamp policy, grading rules, and validation protocol are approved. Real-money rewards should remain disabled until those controls and applicable legal requirements are settled.

Do not promise to remove luck. Outcomes always contain randomness. The defensible goal is to estimate uncertainty, compare forecasts out of sample, and determine whether performance persists after costs and across time.

Decision status

  • Verified: NFL primary sources publish game schedules, player statistics, injury reports, team roster pages, and Next Gen Stats descriptions.
  • Proposed: baseline and injury-aware signal families, correlation analysis, validation gates, and a leaderboard design.
  • Unknown: source access rights, historical snapshots, sportsbook prices, scoring rules, expected reward budget, risk limits, and legal jurisdiction.
  • Not claimed: current picks, a guaranteed edge, any strategy's profitability, or any measured correlation.

Operating principle

One row, one source trail

Research tables link every record to an official source. Unsupported fields are intentionally blank and marked.

Competition principle

Reward repeatable skill

Rank predictive quality and risk-adjusted outcomes on sealed future data—not a short streak or raw hit count.

Important boundary

This site is a design and research artifact

The NFL publishes official stats and injury/roster information, but NFL pages do not provide a complete official sportsbook-odds history or necessarily a documented public depth chart. A verified player stat is not itself evidence of a profitable bet. Until compatible time-stamped odds and historical input snapshots are acquired and licensed, no expected value, correlation, or strategy result can be calculated honestly.
