EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Make decisions auditable.

Start with timestamped, licensed odds and official football data; estimate probabilities before comparing to market prices; log every forecast and lineup snapshot; evaluate against held-out seasons and closing prices. Rank only strategies that clear preregistered statistical, calibration, bankroll-risk, and minimum-sample gates.

Parlays and player-prop combinations require joint probability estimates that account for dependence. Multiplying leg probabilities as if independent is not acceptable without testing.
