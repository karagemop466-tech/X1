Executive summary

A short, decision-focused summary of the project state as of 2026-11-14. Every statement below is backed by a table on this site and a source link.

What was asked

Build a system that originates its own NFL lines, tests strategies against validation gates, ranks them like a competition, removes luck from the results, tracks injuries and depth shortages, and publishes everything with a source link for every number.

What the data says

The market is efficient. Of sixteen pre-registered strategies, fifteen fail the gate suite; the spread model, the totals model, the fade-the-favourite family, the rest-day signal and the backup-quarterback signal all lose to the vig. One signal survives every gate: in outdoor games with sustained wind, the total goes under far more often than the market prices.

The surviving strategy

Bet the under in outdoor games with wind of 12 mph or more. 398 settled out-of-sample bets, +11.53% ROI, 95% bootstrap interval +0.022 to +0.207 units per bet, permutation p = 0.008, and +13.04% ROI after one-score games and blowouts are removed. The effect is present in all four five-year eras, which is the strongest evidence that it is skill rather than luck.

Why the rest failed

Nothing beats the vig after costs. Fading large favourites, betting the home underdog, and the classic cold-weather under thesis all look reasonable and all lose money once the price is applied. The cold-weather thesis is the clearest example: it has no consistent direction across eras, while wind - measured the same way, in the same games - does.

Player props and parlays

Originated prop lines are built and calibrated for passing yards, rushing yards, receiving yards and receptions, with hit rates between 42.8% and 50.3% at the model line. Correlations are measured so that combos can be built intelligently: quarterback passing yards and receiver receiving yards correlate at +0.93 and are effectively the same bet, while running-back rushing and receiving yards correlate at -0.16 and are additive. Parlays fail on arithmetic: two -110 legs need a 27.5% joint hit rate, and the parlay strategies produced 21.4% to 25.8%.

Injuries and depth

The official Week 10 injury report is transcribed row by row from nfl.com (107 rows). Depth tiers are derived from 2026 usage because the league publishes no depth chart; that works for the offensive groups and fails for defensive groups, which is the single largest data gap in the tracker. The measured effect of a missing starter is directionally large but rests on one season, so it is flagged rather than priced.

What needs a decision

Sixteen questions are open and four of them block the next step: which line benchmark is authoritative (Q5), whether the originated line is published raw or as a market difference (Q2), where an authoritative depth chart comes from (Q7), and which prop feed supplies market prices (Q9). Fourteen suggestions are listed with evidence, impact, effort and dependencies so that a path can be chosen before anything is implemented.
