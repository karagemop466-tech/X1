Executive summary

Data as of 2026-11-14 (UTC). Official sources are NFL.com. Figures below are computed from the data files in this repository.

1. What is verified

  • Standings: all 32 rows were re-checked against the NFL.com standings page on 2026-11-14 (W, L, T, PCT, PF, PA, net points, streak). Every value matched. Internal arithmetic also passes.
  • Week 10 schedule: 14 games plus 4 bye teams (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB). The Thursday game is marked final on NFL.com: Commanders 24, Giants 21. The nflverse data agrees.
  • Injury report: 345 rows across 14 games, transcribed from NFL.com. Line-by-line re-check is done for 44 rows (WAS at NYG, NE at DET). The other 301 rows are pending (I-17). Counts by status: Out 66, Doubtful 10, Questionable 84.
  • Spot-checks passed: Mike Evans is listed with the 49ers on his NFL.com player page, matching the injury report.

2. Strategy research: what the numbers say

  • We pre-registered 10 strategies (ATS, totals, rest, and our own Elo model) and tested them on regular-season games from 2006 to 2026 using a fixed holdout of 2021–2025.
  • 0 of 10 strategies pass all gates. In the holdout, the best hit rate is 51.5% (S08 Under total (every game)). Break-even at −110 is 52.4%.
  • Under-totals in the holdout hit 51.5% on 1348 bets. After a Bonferroni correction (×10), that is p = 1.00 (not significant).
  • Our Elo-vs-line strategy (S10) hit 49.4% on 627 holdout bets (ROI -5.6% at −110), below the 52.4% break-even. Its market lines are unverified (I-10).
  • Correlation: mirror pairs (home vs away, over vs under) have phi = ±1. Parlaying them is meaningless, so 14 of 45 pairs are flagged redundant. Among the rest, the largest |phi| is 0.99.
  • Why no strategy passes: gate G0 fails. We cannot verify that the spread and total lines come from a documented, official, or licensed provider (flag I-10). The statistical gates are in the methodology page.

3. Injury and depth tracking

  • We track official injury designations for every listed player in Week 10 (see the injury page for team and unit summaries).
  • Not yet possible: whether a backup replaced a starter. This needs official depth charts, which we have not collected (task T4, blocked). Until then we make no claim about starter-versus-backup deficiencies.
