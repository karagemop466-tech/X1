## Suggestions for the next integration pass

1. **Reconcile dates and scope before polishing UI.** Build review/decision pages from the latest dated register, mark old rows `historical`, `superseded`, `open`, or `resolved`, and require a timestamp/source for a status change.
2. **Make the prospective leaderboard a separate product surface.** Do not expose historical backtest ranks, ROI, rewards, or “passing” badges on it. Keep backtests in a clearly separate research archive, label third-party line inputs, and require a registration hash plus pre-kickoff timestamps for prospective entries.
3. **Treat source tier as a field, not prose.** Every value should carry source class (`official NFL`, `official club`, `third-party mirror`, `model-derived`), capture time, and source URL; only the first two satisfy an official-data claim. A URL alone does not establish an official market price.
4. **Resolve the public/private gate.** Review applicable terms before publishing raw injury/depth snapshots. Until then, publish derived analysis and links to official pages only, and test the exact assembled `_site/` tree for unintended row-level files.
5. **Use a canonical requirements register.** Keep the full owner brief, granular coverage rows, gate, missing quantity/evidence, and what clears it in a single traceable structure. Rebuild the landing and subpages from that structure; do not hand-maintain competing totals.
6. **Keep no-entry honest.** No licensed quote feed, official opening/closing odds, snap-count/participation evidence, or owner-approved reward funding/legal terms were established by this audit. Leave those measurements blank and retain their gates instead of filling them from inference or a mirror.
