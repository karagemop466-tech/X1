Removal of luck

Luck removed three ways: by test, by interval, and by construction.

Built 2026-11-15T18:30:56Z · every row carries its source · blank means not held, never zero
What "removing luck" means here. It does not mean subtracting a number. It means three things: (1) a test that says how extreme a record must be before chance stops explaining it; (2) an interval around every measured rate, so a hot streak cannot be read as an edge; (3) a ranking key that is structurally blind to staking, so a lucky run of bets cannot lift a bad signal.

L2 · A real edge still takes hundreds of bets to see

If a strategy genuinely wins 2 points more often than its break-even, how many settled bets before the 5% test can fire?

Normal approximation to the binomial with the null standard error: n = z^2 p0(1-p0) / (p1-p0)^2 at z = 1.645 (one-sided 5%). This is a planning number, not a result.

L5 · Luck removed by construction: signal and staking scored apart

Of the 16 pre-registered strategies, how many have an interval that excludes zero?

Two different statistics are published per row and they answer two different questions. The p-value is a two-sided exact binomial test of the win rate against the row's own break-even hit rate — it asks whether the picks were better than a break-even picker. The interval is a bootstrap on realised flat-stake ROI — it asks whether the money made is distinguishable from zero. A row can fail one and pass the other, because a pick can beat the break-even win rate and still lose money at the prices actually taken. Neither statistic is substituted for the other.

Summary, both statistics side by side. 16 strategies scored. 7 have a win rate that differs from their own break-even at the 5% level (smallest p = 0.000000, ST-ML-10). 0 have a bootstrap ROI interval that excludes zero, and 0 pass both. The gap between those two counts is the finding. 2 of the 7 beat their break-even win rate and 5 are significantly worse than it — and even the ones that beat it lose money at the prices taken, because the break-even is computed on the average price actually received. Beating 52.38% on paper is not the same as making money. Promotion requires both statistics, so nothing is promoted.
