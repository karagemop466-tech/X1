The three things that block the most

1 · No official price. The league publishes no betting line, and no held source names a book or timestamps a price. Every price-bearing number in this repository is therefore labelled mirror. Closing-line value cannot be computed, and no strategy can be called profitable. (Q-02, blocker)
2 · No snap counts and no inactive lists. "A backup replaced the starter" can be suspected from a depth chart but not confirmed from playing time. Every replacement row says which string steps in; none says the player actually took the snaps. (B-36, B-86, blocker)
3 · The injury report is not yet a time series. Two dated captures exist, which is one delta. A season of dated captures is what makes practice designations replayable. (B-82, in progress)
