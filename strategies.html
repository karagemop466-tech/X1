Strategy Research — Generating Our Own Lines and Useful Signals

1. Philosophy: separating skill from luck

  • Skill = a repeatable decision process whose expected value is positive after accounting for price (the vig) and measured before outcomes are known.
  • Luck = outcome variance. A coin-flip bettor at -110 will win 8+ of 10 weekends regularly; only sample size and closing-line behavior distinguish them from an edge bettor.
  • Luck removal levers (enforced by the validation gates): minimum sample sizes, closing-line value (CLV) as the primary metric, calibration scoring, stability across sub-periods, and multiple-testing control when screening signals.

5. Bet-type handling

  • Single bets: the default. Every pick logged pre-kickoff with line, book, and strategy ID (ledger spec).
  • Parlays: allowed only between games; cross-game legs treated as independent per measured variance ratio (0.99). Correlation caps in props-parlays.html. No same-game parlays until player-level correlations are measured.
  • Player props / prop combos: blocked until play-by-play data exists (OQ-4). Fair lines need usage rates; guessing is prohibited.
